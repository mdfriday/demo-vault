# One Person Company

A one-person company is not “do everything alone forever.” It is **own the critical path** until leverage is proven.

## The critical path

1. Find a pain you can feel
2. Ship a product people pay for (or return to)
3. Keep distribution close to the product
4. Automate or hire only after the loop is boring

> [!tip]
> Your unfair advantage is taste + speed. Protect both.

## Leverage without a team

- Public writing → [[Build in Public]] + [[Personal Branding]]
- Knowledge base → [[Second Brain]] + [[Digital Garden]]
- AI leverage → [[Claude Code]] + [[AI Agents]]

| Hire / buy | Only when |
|------------|-----------|
| Ads | Organic message already converts |
| Contractor | Task is repeatable and specified |
| Full-time | You are the bottleneck weekly |

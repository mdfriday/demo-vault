# Personal Branding

Personal brand is the *promise* people remember when your name shows up. For builders, that promise is usually: **clarity + craft**.

## Make the promise visible

| Surface | Job |
|---------|-----|
| Bio | One sentence promise |
| Garden home | Proof you think in public — [[index]] |
| Product | Promise kept in the UI |
| Content | Same promise, new examples |

> [!quote]
> Brand is what people say when you leave the room — and what they click when you publish.

## Practical moves

- Publish evergreen notes, not only announcements — [[Knowledge Management]]
- Show the work via [[Build in Public]]
- Grow intentionally with [[Audience Building]]

## Best Practices and What to Avoid

- **Best practice:** Move beyond traffic. Design every page to generate real, measurable product action. Assign a "job" and build analysis for every landing page.
- **Best practice:** Attribute and segment all visitors by landing page, content theme, and activation outcome. Use these insights to prioritize new content and iterate on what works.
- **Pitfall:** Vague or generic CTAs lead to high bounce and poor trial or activation conversion. Always match the page to the action the visitor is ready for.
- **Pitfall:** Letting your activation event be too broad (any login, completing a profile) can inflate metrics but won't correlate with customer value or retention.
- **Pitfall:** Relying on homepage as the default organic entry point. Use case-focused landing pages nearly always outperform generic homepages for activation and paid conversion.

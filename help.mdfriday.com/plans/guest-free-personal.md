# Guest / Free / Personal

## Comparison

Numbers match the [pricing page](https://mdfriday.com/pricing/) and the plugin plan cards (v26.10.2).

| Capability | Guest | Free | Personal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positioning | First try | Ongoing free publish | Your domain and long-lived sites |
| Price | Free · no account | Free · sign up | **$5/month** (1 domain) · **$50/year** (1 domain) · **$60/year** (3 domains) |
| Sites | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Storage | **5 MB** | **200 MB** permanent | **5 GB** (account total, not per site) |
| Retention | Cleared at the next **UTC 00:00** | Kept while you stay active. About 6 months of inactivity may archive the site; sign in again to restore. About 30 days after archive, it may be deleted | Same as Free, for as long as the subscription is active |
| First Turnstile | Required | Skipped | Skipped |
| Faithful / single-page / Wiki | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| CDN / share link | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Claim Guest same URL | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Access password | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Custom domain + HTTPS | ✗ | ✗ | Monthly: **1**. Yearly: **1 or 3** |
| Publish history + rollback | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ (last 10 releases) |

Every plan is limited by **storage**, not by how many sites you create. Guest’s **5 MB** is shared across those sites and is sized for trying one note. A second Guest site is allowed until the 5 MB is used up.

> [!tip] Over quota
> Going over the storage quota **blocks new publishes**; already-live sites usually stay reachable (same idea as the website pricing FAQ). Delete or unpublish unused sites, or upgrade.

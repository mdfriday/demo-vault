# How to Build a Personal Brand

## Framework

A personal brand for a builder is **recognizable usefulness**.

1. Pick a lane people can repeat in one sentence.
2. Show receipts (demos, notes, shipped product).
3. Keep a durable home (garden/site) that social feeds cannot delete.
4. Stay consistent on ethos — mine is ownership + one-person leverage + life outside the grind.

## Avoid

- Brand without product
- Product without voice
- Voice that contradicts how you actually work

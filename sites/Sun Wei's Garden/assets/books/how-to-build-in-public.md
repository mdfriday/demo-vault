# How to Build in Public

## Framework

- Share **decisions and demos**, not only moods
- Prefer durable notes (garden) + occasional video
- Protect customer privacy
- Tie posts back to a product promise so strangers know why they should care

## Cadence

Write when you learned something reusable. Record when motion matters. Silence is fine.

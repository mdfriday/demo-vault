# How to Find Your Advantage

Advantage is the intersection of **skill × taste × access to a painful problem**.

## Framework

1. List skills you can exercise for hours without fake motivation.
2. List problems you have personally hated enough to study.
3. List audiences you can already reach (even small).
4. Circle overlaps. That Venn is your starting edge — not your forever prison.

## Anti-patterns

- Copying someone else's niche because it trends
- Mistaking credentials for advantage
- Claiming "AI" as an edge without a workflow

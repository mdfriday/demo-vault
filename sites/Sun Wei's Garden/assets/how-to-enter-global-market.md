# How to Enter Global Market

## Framework

1. **Language of the product** — clear English beats clever slang.
2. **Payment & packaging** — assume international buyers early when it fits.
3. **Distribution venues** — go where the pain is discussed globally.
4. **Support style** — screenshots, specifics, timezone honesty.
5. **One motion** — a one-person company rarely wins five localizations at once.

## Checklist

- [ ] Can a stranger understand the promise in one screen?
- [ ] Does your site load and read cleanly worldwide?
- [ ] Are examples culturally portable (vaults, sites, not only local platforms)?

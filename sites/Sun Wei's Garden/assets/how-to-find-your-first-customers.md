# How to Find Your First Customers

## Framework

1. Talk to people who already tried the painful workaround.
2. Demo with *their* materials when possible.
3. Ask for money after value is visible, not after your ego is ready.
4. Write down objections; they are product tickets.
5. Stop chasing audiences who want a different job-to-be-done.

## Script skeleton

- What did you last try?
- What broke or felt heavy?
- If this worked, what would you publish first?

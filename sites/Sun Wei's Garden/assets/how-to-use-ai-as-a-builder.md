# How to Use AI as a Builder

## Framework

Treat AI as a **junior collaborator with infinite stamina**, not as the owner of architecture.

### Good uses
- Drafting docs and garden notes you will edit
- Exploring codebases faster (still read the critical paths)
- Generating boilerplate, tests, refactors under your review
- Summarizing customer conversations into candidate tickets

### Bad uses
- Outsourcing product taste
- Shipping code you cannot explain
- Letting the model invent customer quotes or metrics

## Pair with leverage design

[[Four Hour Workday]] only works if AI removes toil without removing understanding. Same for [[Building Foundry]]: AI accelerates, you remain accountable.

## My Own Example

I use AI daily while building [[MDFriday]], writing this garden, and preparing demos — always with a human final pass on promises made to customers.

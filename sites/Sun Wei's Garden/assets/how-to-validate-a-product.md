# How to Validate a Product

## Framework

Validation is evidence that someone outside your head cares enough to act.

Signals (strong → weaker):

1. Payment
2. Repeated voluntary use
3. Introductions to peers with the same pain
4. Detailed objections (they cared enough to argue)
5. Compliments with no behavior (ignore as primary evidence)

## Methods

- Concierge demos before perfect onboarding
- Waitlists only if you can learn *why* people join
- Ship a narrow wedge — [[Why Focus on Publish]] energy

# Own Your Knowledge

**Own Your Knowledge. Build Your Business. Enjoy Your Life.**

The first sentence is the root. If your notes only exist inside a rented interface, you are renting your compound interest.

I return to this line when product decisions get noisy. Features are infinite. Ownership is a filter.

## What ownership means here

- Files on disk you can copy
- Formats that travel (Markdown)
- Publish paths that don't demand surrendering the source
- A garden you can move between tools if needed
- Backups you control, not only "trust us" dashboards

Ownership does not mean isolation. You can still use clouds, CDNs, and collaborators. It means the source of truth is not trapped.

## Why it pairs with MDFriday

[[MDFriday]] exists to help knowledge creators publish without giving up that ownership. Local-first is not nostalgia; it is business continuity for your brain. Selective publish is how ownership stays compatible with a public voice — you choose what leaves the private vault.

## Why it pairs with a one-person company

When you are small, your knowledge *is* the company in many early seasons. Protect it. Share it intentionally. Monetize the product around it without locking the thinking into a silo. See [[One Person Company]] and [[Why MDFriday]].

## Why the third sentence matters

Enjoy Your Life is not anti-ambition. It is anti-martyrdom. A knowledge business that destroys the knower is a bad trade. Ownership includes owning your time.

## A test I use

If a tool disappeared tomorrow, could I still open my notes in a plain editor and rebuild a site? If the answer is no, I do not fully own the knowledge — I am leasing a feeling of productivity.

# First 10 Paying Customers

The first ten paying customers did not arrive because I ran a clever funnel. They arrived because I talked to people who already felt the pain, showed a path that respected their vault, and asked for money only after the path was real.

## Who they were

Most early customers looked like a mix of:

- Independent consultants who publish frameworks from Obsidian
- Indie hackers who want a site without maintaining a second CMS
- Educators / course builders who think in Markdown
- A few power users who had already tried DIY Hugo/Quartz setups and wanted less friction

They were not "anyone who writes." They were people for whom **publishing is part of the business**, not a hobby tab.

## What actually converted

1. **Clarity of the promise** — publish from notes you already trust. See [[Why Focus on Publish]].
2. **Respect for local-first** — files stay yours; the product helps them appear on the web.
3. **Selective publish** — nobody wants to accidentally ship their entire private life.
4. **Human conversation** — early sales were closer to consulting than self-serve magic. That taught me [[Learning Sales]].

## What did not convert

Beautiful landing-page poetry without a demo. Feature lists that sounded like a CMS. Competing on "hosted convenience" alone. People who wanted a social network, not a site.

## Lessons I keep

- Ten customers teach more than ten thousand impressions.
- Pricing is a product decision: it filters for seriousness.
- Global users showed up earlier than I expected — which pushed [[Serving Global Users]].
- Building in public helped, but only when the public notes were useful. See [[Building in Public]] and [[How to Build in Public]].

## Methods for others

If you want a reusable checklist: [[How to Find Your First Customers]] and [[How to Validate a Product]].

MDFriday's early revenue did not prove I had "won." It proved the problem is real enough that strangers will pay to reduce friction. That is enough oxygen to keep shipping [[MDFriday Publish]] and the deeper work in [[Building Foundry]].

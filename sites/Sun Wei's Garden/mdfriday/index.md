# MDFriday

**MDFriday** is my product journey: local-first publishing for knowledge creators.

I built it because I wanted Obsidian notes to become a real site without surrendering ownership to a hosted-only silo, and without turning every publish into a weekend of CLI archaeology. MDFriday is for people who already think in Markdown — writers, consultants, indie hackers, educators — and want a calm path from vault → web.

## Living Timeline

### 2024–2025 — The itch
I kept hitting the same wall: my best thinking lived in Obsidian, but the public web wanted a different toolchain. I explored Hugo deeply (see [[Why I Read Hugo Source Code]]) and realized the gap wasn't "another theme" — it was *publish UX for note people*.

### Early 2026 — Focus on publish
I narrowed the promise: **publish**, not "be a full CMS". That decision still guides the roadmap — [[Why Focus on Publish]], [[Why MDFriday]].

### Mid 2026 — First revenue
Conversations became customers. The story is not a growth-hack fairy tale; it's awkward demos, clearer packaging, and listening — [[First 10 Paying Customers]], [[Serving Global Users]].

### Late summer 2026 — Foundry & architecture
Reading Hugo wasn't academic. It led to [[Building Foundry]] and a clearer [[MDFriday Technical Architecture]]. The product surface people touch day-to-day is [[MDFriday Publish]].

### Sep 2026 — Now
Global market focus, AI-assisted building, and a public garden that dogfoods the product. Status snapshot: [[Latest Status]].

# Latest Status

**Date:** September 2026

## MDFriday right now

- **Positioning:** local-first publish for knowledge creators (Obsidian + Markdown), selective by design.
- **Market focus:** global users, English-first public site (sunwei.xyz), bilingual life behind the scenes — [[Serving Global Users]].
- **Core bet:** [[MDFriday Publish]] stays sharp; [[Building Foundry]] keeps deepening compatibility and reliability.
- **Company mode:** [[One Person Company]] with AI leverage — not a fake "team of fifty" story.

## Shipping posture

I am optimizing for consistent weekly progress over theatrical launches. Public writing and [[YouTube]] support distribution, but the product still has to reduce publish friction in the hands of a real vault owner. If a feature cannot be explained in a garden note or shown in a two-minute demo, it is probably not ready to advertise.

This month's practical focus is polish on the publish path people actually touch: selection clarity, preview trust, and deploy paths that do not require a second career in DevOps. Architecture work continues underneath — see [[MDFriday Technical Architecture]] — but I am refusing to confuse "interesting engine work" with "customer value shipped."

## What I'm watching

- Whether new users understand selective publish in the first session
- Theme + hosting paths that feel "done enough" for a serious personal site
- Sales conversations repeating the same objections (those become docs and product work)
- Whether this garden itself stays maintainable — if I can't garden, the product thesis is lying
- How AI changes my throughput without changing my taste — [[How to Use AI as a Builder]]

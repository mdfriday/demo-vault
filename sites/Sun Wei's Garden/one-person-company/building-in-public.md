# Building in Public

Building in public is not posting every emotion. It is publishing **useful residue** from the work: decisions, tradeoffs, demos, and lessons that a stranger could reuse.

I treat public building as a discipline for a [[One Person Company]]. Without a marketing department, artifacts have to compound. A garden note about publish focus still works months later. A vague motivational post does not.

## What I share

- Product decisions like [[Why Focus on Publish]]
- Technical narratives like [[Why I Read Hugo Source Code]] and [[Building Foundry]]
- Customer-learning notes (without leaking private details)
- Process experiments like [[Four Hour Workday]]
- Status that is allowed to be boringly true — [[Latest Status]]

I also share unfinished thinking when it is labeled honestly. Gardens allow seedlings. The point is not to look certain; the point is to leave a trail of reasoning.

## What I don't share

Secrets that harm customers. Fake vanity metrics. Performative suffering. "Behind the scenes" content that is only anxiety with better lighting. Internal financial details that help nobody and distract everybody.

## Why it helps

Public artifacts create discovery while I sleep. They also create accountability. If I wrote that selective publish matters, I should still ship like it matters. Building in public pairs with [[Learning Marketing]] without forcing me to become a full-time creator.

## Channels

This [[Digital Garden]] is the durable home. [[YouTube]] is for motion and demos. Occasional posts elsewhere are invitations back to the garden or the product. Social feeds are weather; the garden is climate.

## Failure modes I watch for

- Performing transparency instead of shipping
- Teaching generic hustle with no product spine
- Publishing so much process that I avoid the scary customer conversation
- Optimizing for applause from builders who will never buy

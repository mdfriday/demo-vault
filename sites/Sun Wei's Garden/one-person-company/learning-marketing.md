# Learning Marketing

Marketing is how I earn attention without pretending I'm a media company.

For a solo founder, marketing is mostly the disciplined repetition of a true promise in places where the right strangers already gather. It is not a costume. It is not a personality transplant.

## What works for me

- Teaching notes in this garden
- Honest comparisons of approaches (local-first vs DIY vs hosted-only) without cartoon villains
- [[YouTube]] when a demo needs motion
- Showing up where knowledge creators already talk about publish pain
- Repeating the same clear promise until it feels almost too simple: publish for knowledge creators
- Pointing people to durable pages like [[Why MDFriday]] and [[MDFriday Publish]]

## What I avoid

Growth hacks that train the wrong audience. Posting schedules that empty the vault. "Content calendars" that force me to invent problems I do not have. Marketing that contradicts [[Own Your Knowledge]] by pushing people into rented-only thinking. Brand shouting — it is always **MDFriday**, never mangled casing.

## How marketing relates to sales

Marketing warms recognition; sales creates agreement. If marketing is vague, sales becomes education from zero every time. If marketing is specific, sales can focus on fit and next steps — [[Learning Sales]]. Building in public is the bridge between them — [[Building in Public]].

## Global note

Because I am aiming at global users — [[Serving Global Users]] — I write main bodies in clear English and keep examples portable. The site is sunwei.xyz facing the world. Short Chinese may appear where my voice needs it (as on [[Now]]), but the product story should travel.

# Building in Public on YouTube

YouTube building-in-public means narrating real work: shipping [[MDFriday Publish]], wrestling architecture, learning sales — without turning every private struggle into content debt.

The camera is a commitment device. If I say I will demo selective publish, I must make selective publish demoable. That pressure improves the product when I use it carefully, and destroys focus when I use it as performance.

## Cadence philosophy

Ship video when there is a demo or decision worth preserving. Otherwise write a note here. The garden is durable; the feed is weather. I would rather have twelve useful videos and a living garden than two hundred empty uploads.

## What "public" includes

- Roadmap reasoning at a high level
- UX walkthroughs
- Lessons from [[First Customer]] style stories with privacy preserved
- Architecture narratives like Foundry when they help users understand tradeoffs

## What "public" excludes

Private customer data. Security-sensitive details. Rage content. Fake rivalries. Anything that would make me hesitant to open the vault the next morning.

## Relationship to the company

YouTube supports [[One Person Company]] distribution and [[Learning Marketing]], but it reports to the product, not the other way around. If video starts eating [[Four Hour Workday]] leverage with no customer value, I cut volume.

## Collaboration with the garden

Every video should leave a note behind — even a short one — so the learning is not trapped in a platform. Prefer linking Journey pages like [[Latest Status]] or Assets like [[How to Build in Public]] rather than inventing disposable descriptions only on YouTube.

## Ethos check on camera

Before publishing a video I ask: does this help someone own their knowledge, build their business, or enjoy their life a little more clearly? If it is only engagement bait, it waits.

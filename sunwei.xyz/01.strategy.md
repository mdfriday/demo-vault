# Strategy

## Self-positioning

> A programmer who loves technology, building his own business with the help of AI.
> Using technology and AI to build assets, so that work gradually shifts from trading time for income to assets creating income.

## Altruistic mindset

Honestly show my own transformation: the journey from employee to independent entrepreneur.

Technologist + Builder + Entrepreneur

> Own your knowledge.
> Build your business.
> Enjoy your life.

Build in public, and turn my personal website into a Digital Garden. Digital Gardens emerged because people began to realize they need to own their own intellectual property, and to let that knowledge keep growing.


## Platform positioning

- sunwei.xyz: Knowledge. Show the real process of how knowledge grows while building a one-person company, like a documentary.
- mdfriday.com: Showcase products and services, helping users solve problems.
  - Vision: Help people turn knowledge into businesses.
  - Product positioning: Build websites that help knowledge creators grow, sell, and serve.
- YouTube: Show the emotional ups and downs of the whole process, and the fun things that happen along the way. Also make some product-related videos, which are part of the real process too.

## Core idea

Let doing replace talking. Through the doing, let people get to know me little by little, staying consistent and authentic.

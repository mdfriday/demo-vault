# Current Stage

MDFriday Publish has just entered the international market.

This is not the growth stage.

Not the scaling stage.

Not the revenue-optimization stage.

The one and only goal right now:

## Find the first 10 international users who genuinely need MDFriday.

At this stage:

- Understanding users > number of features
- Number of conversations > amount of traffic
- Product direction > revenue optimization
- Speed of learning > speed of development

---

# The System

The whole system has only three layers:

```text
MDFriday
    ↓
Learning
    ↓
SunWei.xyz
    ↓
Distribution
    ↓
Users
    ↓
Feedback
    ↓
MDFriday
```

---

# Layer 1 — MDFriday

## Purpose

Build a real product.

## Focus

Answer one question:

"Why would someone choose MDFriday instead of their current publishing workflow?"

## Outputs

- Product
- Features
- Experiments
- Releases
- User feedback
- Usage data

## Success Metric

Not:

- Revenue
- MRR

But:

- Active users
- First publish completed
- Repeat publishing
- User conversations

---

# Layer 2 — SunWei.xyz

## Purpose

Turn experience into assets.

## Structure

```text
SunWei.xyz
│
├── index.md
├── /building
├── /knowledge
├── /skills
├── /books
├── /one-person-company
└── /mdfriday
```

## Meaning

### index.md

Homepage.

Current focus.

What I'm building now.

---

### /building

Build in Public log.

Records:

- What I did this week
- Why I did it
- What I learned
- What's next

This is the entry point of the whole system.

---

### /knowledge

Validated knowledge.

For example:

- User research
- Product design
- SaaS
- AI
- Markdown Publishing

The point:

Knowledge.

Not logs.

---

### /skills

Reusable capabilities.

Distilled from knowledge.

Goal:

Something AI and my future self can use directly.

For example:

```text
User Interview
Competitor Analysis
Product Positioning
MVP Validation
Build In Public
```

Knowledge → Skills

---

### /books

Books I've written.

Records:

- Technical books I've written
- Books on one-person companies
- Other books

A digital asset for my personal books.
It also builds authority.

---

### /one-person-company

Entrepreneurship in practice.

For example:

- Pricing
- Customer acquisition
- Sales
- Business models
- Freelancing

---

### /mdfriday

Product knowledge base.

Includes:

- Vision
- Product Map
- Roadmap
- User Research
- Decisions

This is MDFriday's long-term asset.

# Layer 3 — Distribution

## Purpose

Get feedback.

Not create content for content's sake.

---

## Reddit

### Role

Discussion.

### Goal

Understand users.

### Content

- Pain points
- Workflows
- Questions
- Product demos
- Feedback requests

### Success Metric

Not:

- Views

But:

- Comments
- Conversations
- DM
- Follow-up discussions

Reddit is the most important platform right now.

---

## YouTube

### Role

Trust.

### Goal

Show real work.

### Content

- Product demos
- User learnings
- Product decisions
- One-person-company journey

### Success Metric

Not view count.

But:

- Users get to know you
- Users understand MDFriday
- Users are willing to try it

---

## Future Platforms

Possible future expansion:

- X
- Indie Hackers
- Hacker News

But they're not the focus right now.

First get Reddit + YouTube working.

---

# Content Flow

Don't create content.

Let content come out of real work.

```text
User Conversation
        ↓
Learning
        ↓
Product Decision
        ↓
MDFriday Change
        ↓
Building Note
        ↓
Knowledge
        ↓
YouTube
        ↓
More Users
```

One real event produces multiple assets.

---

# Weekly Rhythm

## Monday

Research

Question:

What am I trying to learn?

Output:

Weekly User Research

---

## Tuesday

Conversations

Goal:

At least 5 meaningful conversations

Channels:

- Reddit
- Obsidian Forum
- GitHub

Questions:

- How are you publishing today?
- What is frustrating?
- Why this workflow?

---

## Wednesday

Build

Principle:

Solve one visible user problem.

Don't build:

The coolest feature.

Build:

The feature that most affects users' decisions.

---

## Thursday

Demo

Show results.

Not promote results.

Output:

- Short video
- GIF
- Screenshot

The point:

User outcomes

Not technical implementation

---

## Friday

Release

Publish:

- Plugin
- Changelog
- GitHub Release

At the same time, record:

- Why it was built
- Which user feedback triggered it
- What I learned

---

## Saturday

Building Note

Publish to:

/building

Template:

```md
# This Week Building MDFriday

## What I expected

## What actually happened

## What I learned

## What changed my mind

## What I'm doing next
```

The point:

Learning

Not Progress

---

## Sunday

Review

Look at:

### Awareness

- Reddit Views
- Website Visits

### Interest

- Comments
- GitHub Stars
- Community Discussions

### Activation

- Plugin Install
- First Publish

### Retention

- Weekly Active Users
- Repeat Publish

### Revenue

- Trial
- Paid User

---

# Weekly Success Criteria

A successful week:

- 10+ user conversations
- 1 useful release
- 1 demo
- 2 community discussions
- 1 building note
- 1 documented learning

A failed week:

- Wrote a lot of code
- Didn't talk to users

---

# North Star

Don't optimize for now:

- Revenue
- MRR
- Pricing

Optimize for now:

## Why do people choose MDFriday instead of their current workflow?

Until the answer becomes very clear.

---

# Guiding Principle

Don't ask:

"What should I post today?"

Ask:

"What did I learn this week that is worth remembering?"

Don't ask:

"How do I get more followers?"

Ask:

"How do I better understand the people I'm building for?"

Don't ask:

"What content should I create?"

Ask:

"What experience can I turn into an asset?"

---

Build MDFriday.

Document the journey.

Own the knowledge.

Let distribution happen naturally.

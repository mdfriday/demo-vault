Most competitors make pricing complicated. For MDFriday, I want to keep it simple.

Your site is built locally. Our real costs come mainly from storage and bandwidth. At this stage, storage is the only significant fixed cost. So we price based on storage, not the number of sites.

**Unlimited sites. Pay for storage. Simple.**

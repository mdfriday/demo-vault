# Building

The Build in Public log, and the entry point of the whole system. Every Saturday I publish a Building Note that records what I did this week, why I did it, what I learned, and what's next. The focus is **Learning**, not Progress.

> [!tip] Let content come out of real work
> User Conversation → Learning → Product Decision → MDFriday Change → **Building Note** → Knowledge → YouTube → More Users
>
> Lessons worth keeping for the long term go into [[knowledge/index|Knowledge]].

## Conventions

```text
Building Note
├── Context       → The problem I'm solving
├── What Happened → What I did this week
├── What I Learned → The insight I gained
├── What Changed My Mind → Why I changed my assumptions
├── What I'm Doing Next → The next step I'm taking
│
└── Content Distribution
    ├── Reddit       → A in-depth discussion with users
    ├── Indie Hackers → business lesson
    ├── YouTube      → Showcase + story
    └── MDFriday     → Product / SEO / tutorial / comparison
```

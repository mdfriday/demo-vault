## Twitter Marketing

### Strategy
- **Real-time engagement** with trending topics
- **Share thoughts, insights, expertise**
- **Build personal brand and thought leadership**
- **Drive traffic through shared links**
- **Engage with industry conversations**

### Key Tactics

**The "Poster Boy" Formula**:
- Mention influencers/authors
- Reference results from reading their content
- Show testimonial/praise
- Provide value back to them
- They're incentivized to retweet (social proof)

**Example Tweet**:
"Just finished @[Author]'s book on email marketing. Implemented their 'subject line framework' and got 45% open rate increase. This changed everything for my campaigns!"

### Best Practices
- Tweet 1-2 times daily
- Use relevant hashtags (3-5 per tweet)
- Engage with replies within 1 hour
- Retweet/reply to industry leaders
- Share original insights, not just links
- Use thread format for in-depth topics
- Include images (tweets with images get 2x engagement)

### Metrics to Track
- Retweets (people sharing your content)
- Favorites/Likes
- Clicks to website
- Follower growth rate

## Instagram Marketing

### Strategy
- **Visual storytelling** of your brand
- **Behind-the-scenes content** builds connection
- **Showcase products/results** in beautiful way
- **Build engaged community** through daily posts
- **Long-term brand awareness** and trust

### Content Pillars (Instagram)
- 40% Educational content (teach, tips)
- 30% Behind-the-scenes (humanize brand)
- 20% User-generated content (showcase customers)
- 10% Promotional (sales, offers)

### Key Features

**Stories** (Ephem eral)
- Post daily
- Build urgency (disappears in 24 hours)
- Polls, questions, interactive elements
- Drive engagement

**Reels** (Short videos)
- Algorithm-favored (get more reach)
- 15-90 second videos
- Entertaining or educational
- Higher visibility than regular posts

**Posts** (Permanent)
- High-quality images/graphics
- Carousel posts (multiple images)
- Video posts
- Captions with hashtags

### Instagram Strategy
- Post 4-5 times per week
- Stories daily
- Optimal times: 11 AM and 7-9 PM
- Use 20-30 hashtags
- Engage with followers' content
- Respond to DMs within 2 hours
- Collaborate with other accounts

### Building Followers
- Post consistently
- Use trending sounds (Reels)
- Engage authentically
- Use relevant hashtags
- Share follower content
- Run engagement campaigns

## LinkedIn Marketing

### Strategy
- **Professional networking** and relationship building
- **Thought leadership** in your industry
- **B2B lead generation**
- **Personal brand** as professional
- **Industry insights and knowledge sharing**

### Best Content
- Professional articles (long-form)
- Career advice and industry insights
- Company culture posts
- Case studies and results
- Professional development tips
- Industry trends and analysis

### LinkedIn Best Practices
- Post 2-3 times per week
- Optimal time: Tuesday-Thursday, 8 AM
- Use long-form posts (algorithm favors depth)
- Ask questions (increase engagement)
- Share others' content (build network)
- Use hashtags (3-5)
- LinkedIn articles (establish expertise)

### Lead Generation on LinkedIn
- Connect with ideal customers
- Personalized connection messages
- Share valuable content
- Respond to comments/messages
- Offer free resources
- Direct to email signup
- Build trust before selling

## Pinterest Marketing

### Strategy
- **Discovery platform** (different from social media)
- **Drive traffic to website** (main goal)
- **Evergreen content** (pins work forever)
- **Visual inspiration** for lifestyle/business topics
- **Long-term, sustainable traffic source**

### How Pinterest Works
- Users search for ideas (like Google)
- Save pins to boards
- Pins drive traffic to source websites
- One pin can drive traffic for years

### Pinterest Strategy
- Create vertical pins (1000x1500 pixels)
- Write keyword-rich pin titles
- Include descriptions
- Link pins to blog posts (traffic generator)
- Create multiple pins per content (5-10 variations)
- Post consistently (5-10 pins daily)
- Use Rich Pins (product, recipe pins)

### Best Practices
- Use trending keywords in pin text
- Test different pin designs
- Link to SEO-optimized content
- Create pin graphics for every blog post
- Use Canva for easy creation
- Monitor top-performing pins
- Repin high-performing pins weekly

### Pinterest Timeline
- Slower than other platforms
- Takes 2-3 months to see real traffic
- But highly sustainable long-term
- Old pins continue driving traffic

## Choosing Your Platforms

### Recommended Starting Stack

**For Personal Brand/Thought Leadership**:
- Twitter (engagement)
- LinkedIn (professional)
- One visual platform (Instagram or Pinterest)

**For B2B**:
- LinkedIn (primary)
- Twitter (engagement)
- Email list building

**For Consumer Products**:
- Instagram (visual)
- Pinterest (discovery)
- Facebook (community)
- TikTok (young audiences)

**For Content Creators**:
- YouTube (main platform)
- Instagram Stories/Reels (cross-promotion)
- Twitter (engagement)
- Email list (owned audience)

### Resource Allocation

Don't try all platforms. Choose 2-3 and dominate.

**Quality > Quantity**

- 100 consistent posts on Twitter > 10 posts each on 5 platforms
- Master one platform first
- Expand to second platform
- Add third platform only when established

## Cross-Platform Strategy

### Content Repurposing

Create once, publish everywhere:

1. **Write blog post**
2. Create 5-10 social clips from post
3. Post on Twitter (multiple formats)
4. Create Pinterest pins (link back)
5. Instagram Reels/Stories
6. LinkedIn article version
7. Email newsletter

**Result**: 1 piece of content → 50+ social posts → Massive reach

### Platform-Specific Adaptation

Same message, different format:

- **Blog Post**: Long-form, 1,500+ words
- **Twitter**: Quote or tip from post
- **LinkedIn**: Professional angle with insights
- **Instagram**: Visual summary with story
- **Pinterest**: Beautiful pin + link
- **Email**: Personal message + link

## Common Mistakes Across Platforms

❌ Post same content across platforms (doesn't work)
❌ No engagement/interaction (broadcasting not connecting)
❌ Inconsistent posting (algorithms forget you)
❌ No clear call-to-action (don't know what you want)
❌ Ugly graphics/low quality (reflects poorly)
❌ Ignore comments/messages (rude and hurts algorithm)
❌ Sell immediately (build trust first)
❌ Follow growth over engagement (fake followers worthless)
❌ No link tracking (can't measure ROI)
❌ Give up after 2 months (takes time to see results)

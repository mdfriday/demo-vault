## Channel Summary Matrix

**Channel Type**: Owned  
**Timeline**: Medium

**Key Metric**: Open rate, click rate  
**Module**: [[04-Email-Marketing]]

**Channel Type**: Owned + Organic

**ROI**: Very high (free traffic)

**Channel Type**: Owned + Organic

## Success Metrics Tracker

Track these metrics for each channel:

| Metric | Meaning | Target | Channel |
|--------|---------|--------|---------|
| **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)** | How much to get one customer | Low is better | All paid |
| **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)** | Revenue ÷ Ad spend | 3:1+ | All paid |
| **Open Rate** | % who open email | 20-30% | Email |
| **Click-Through Rate** | % who click link | 1-5% | Ads, email |
| **Conversion Rate** | % who take action | 2-10% | Depends on offer |
| **Cost Per Click** | Cost of each click | $0.50-$5 | Ads |
| **Cost Per Lead** | Cost of email signup | $0.50-$5 | Lead gen ads |
| **Watch Time** | Total minutes watched | Higher better | YouTube |
| **Engagement Rate** | Interactions % | 1-5% | Social |
| **Organic Reach** | Free people reached | Higher better | Organic |

## Testing & Optimization Workflow

For **Every Channel**, follow this framework:

### Phase 1: Launch (Week 1-2)
- Set baseline metrics
- Identify underperforming elements
- Collect data
- Document current state

### Phase 2: Optimize (Week 3-8)
- A/B test one variable at a time
- Scale winners
- Pause losers
- Document improvements

### Phase 3: Scale (Month 2+)
- Increase budget on winners
- Expand to similar audiences
- Test complementary channels
- Build processes for scaling

## The Complete Marketing Funnel

### Top of Funnel (Awareness)
**Goal**: Get noticed  
**Channels**: Content, SEO, YouTube, Social Media  
**Metrics**: Reach, impressions, views

### Middle of Funnel (Consideration)
**Goal**: Build interest and trust  
**Channels**: Email, Quora, Reviews, Content  
**Metrics**: Click rate, engagement, time on site

### Bottom of Funnel (Decision)
**Goal**: Drive conversion  
**Channels**: Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Email, Website  
**Metrics**: Conversion rate, cost per conversion

### Post-Purchase (Retention)
**Goal**: Repeat revenue and referrals  
**Channels**: Email, Community, Social, Support  
**Metrics**: Repeat purchase rate, referrals, LTV

## Troubleshooting Quick Reference

### Problem: Low Email Open Rate
**Diagnosis**: 
- Subject line not compelling
- Sender email not trustworthy
- Sending time wrong
- Wrong list segmentation

**Solution**:
- Test new subject line formulas
- Use business email (not Gmail)
- Test different send times
- Segment by engagement

### Problem: Low Website Traffic
**Diagnosis**:
- Content not ranking
- Not enough content
- Low-quality content
- Wrong keywords targeted

**Solution**:
- Add more content (10-20 pages)
- Improve on-page SEO
- Target easier keywords first
- Build backlinks

### Problem: Low Conversion Rate
**Diagnosis**:
- Poor landing page
- Wrong audience
- Unclear value proposition
- Too many form fields

**Solution**:
- Improve headline/copy
- Refine audience targeting
- Strengthen social proof
- Reduce form to 3 fields

### Problem: High Ad Cost
**Diagnosis**:
- Low quality score
- Broad audience
- Poor keyword match
- Irrelevant landing page

**Solution**:
- Improve ad relevance
- Narrow audience
- Use exact match keywords
- Optimize landing page

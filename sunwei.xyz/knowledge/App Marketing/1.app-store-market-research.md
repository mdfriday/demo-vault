# 1. App Store Market Research

## What is This?

This note summarizes the first step of app marketing: research the market, competitors, keywords, and user needs before and after launch.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Market research is essential both before launch and during operation
- Research targets include: competitors, category dynamics, keywords, reviews, user pain points
- Research determines promotion direction and positioning strategy

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Without understanding competitive landscape and user needs, promotion budgets are easily wasted
- The app store itself is a database of user needs and market feedback

## MASTER | Execution

- Study competitor positioning and review feedback
- Identify differentiation opportunities and key value propositions
- Use research findings to inform app description, screenshots, and promotional messaging

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Have I researched main competitors?
- [ ] Do I understand what target users care most about?
- [ ] Have I found my differentiation positioning?

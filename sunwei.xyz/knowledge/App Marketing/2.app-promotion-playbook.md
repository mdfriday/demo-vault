## What is This?

This note summarizes overall app promotion strategy: not relying on a single channel, but combining multiple promotion methods to achieve initial momentum.

## KNOW | Foundation

- App promotion channels are typically numerous: content, social media, communities, PR, recommendations, email, partnerships, etc.
- Promotion goals include not just download numbers, but retention, word-of-mouth, and viral spread

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Single-channel promotion is volatile; multi-channel is more stable
- Early promotion is often more like "cold-start operations" than pure ad buying

## MASTER | Execution

- List available promotion channels and rank by cost/speed
- Prioritize low-cost validation promotion first
- Observe which channels bring higher-quality users

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Do I have multi-channel promotion perspective?
- [ ] Do I distinguish between download volume and quality users?
- [ ] Am I doing low-cost validation first?

# 3. Using Paid Ads for App Growth

## What is This?

This note summarizes paid user acquisition strategy in app promotion, particularly how Facebook Ads and Google Ads contribute to app growth.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Paid advertising works best when amplifying already-validated growth paths
- Common platforms include Facebook Ads and Google Ads
- Ad success metrics should include retention and quality, not just installs

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Without proper app positioning and store page optimization, ads amplify problems instead of results
- Only when you understand which users have higher lifetime value does targeting become effective

## MASTER | Execution

- Start with clear audience definition and value proposition
- Test ad creative, audiences, and conversion paths
- Track CPI (cost per install) AND retention/behavior metrics

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Have I validated base product and positioning first?
- [ ] Do I have clear target user personas?
- [ ] Am I measuring quality metrics, not just download volume?

# App Marketing

> This knowledge base organizes the App Marketing course into a system focusing on **market research, promotion channels, and paid app advertising**.

## Core Philosophy

App marketing success starts by confirming the market opportunity and competitive landscape, then using multi-channel promotion to achieve initial installs and word-of-mouth, and finally leveraging ads to scale quality user acquisition.

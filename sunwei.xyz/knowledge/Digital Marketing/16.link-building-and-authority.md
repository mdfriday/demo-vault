# 16. Link Building & Authority Accumulation

## What is This?

Link building is a primary expression of "authority" in SEO. Simply: When other websites link to you, search engines are more likely to view you as a trustworthy source.

## KNOW | Foundation

### Why Links Matter

The course is clear: Rankings depend on relevance AND authority, and links are a primary authority signal.

### Not All Links Are Equal

Distinguish between:

- **Dofollow links** - More likely to pass SEO value
- **Nofollow links** - Mainly for referral traffic/exposure, weaker SEO weight

Also consider the quality and authority of the source domain itself.

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

### Link Building Isn't "Distribute Links Everywhere"

Truly valuable links typically come from:
- High-quality websites
- Topic-related websites
- Natural mention within article context
- Because others believe your content deserves citation

### Smart Starting Point: Study How Competitors Get Links

The course provides practical methodology:
- Examine what external links your top competitors have
- Figure out how they got those links
- Assess whether you can replicate or improve on it

Common sources include:
- Guest posts
- Resource pages
- Brand mentions  
- List articles
- Case studies/success stories
- Tool recommendations

## MASTER | Execution

### Actionable Link Building Methods

1. **Competitor link analysis** - Research before acting blindly
2. **Outreach to potential citers** - Email relevant people with content recommendations
3. **Unlinked brand mentions** - Ask sites that mention your brand to turn it into a link
4. **Shareable content formats** - Lists, surveys, expert roundups, infographics
5. **Leverage high-authority platforms** - YouTube, Quora, Slides, etc. to gain initial reach and weight
6. **Poster Boy Formula** - Create success case studies that others naturally want to share

### Link-Building Priority

Focus first on:
- Relevance (does the link source match your topic?)
- Quality (is the source domain authoritative?)
- Natural context (is this mentioned genuinely?)
- Sustainable relationships (can you build this long-term?)

Not quantity alone.

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Have I analyzed competitor link sources?
- [ ] Do I distinguish between traffic value and SEO value?
- [ ] Am I creating content worth citing?
- [ ] Have I actively reached out for links?
- [ ] Am I using high-authority platforms for early exposure?

# 17. Local SEO & Review Systems

## What is This?

If your business has a physical location, service area, or local customer base, Local SEO works differently than traditional SEO. It depends more on data completeness, geographic relevance, and review systems.

## KNOW | Foundation

### Local SEO Applies To

- Restaurants and cafes
- Fitness gyms and studios
- Hotels and accommodations
- Local service providers
- Brick-and-mortar retailers
- Any location-based business

### Key Platforms Emphasized by the Course

- Google Business Profile
- Yelp
- Facebook Page
- TripAdvisor (especially for travel and destinations)

### Why Local Search Is Powerful

Local search users typically:
- Are closer to purchase decision
- Move faster to action
- Search on mobile
- Are influenced by maps, star ratings, hours

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

### Local SEO Isn't About Content—It's "Complete Data + Reviews + Consistency"

For local businesses, ranking and clicks depend on:
- Complete business information
- Consistent address and phone across platforms
- Real, positive reviews
- Accurate operating hours
- Platform recognition as active and trustworthy

### NAP Consistency Is Critical

NAP = Name / Address / Phone

Inconsistency across platforms signals untrustworthiness to search engines.

Standardize across all platforms:
- Business name spelling/format
- Address format
- Phone number
- Operating hours
- Website link

### Review Systems Impact Both Rankings AND Conversions

In local search results, users first notice:
- Star rating
- Number of reviews
- Recency of reviews
- Whether business responds to negative reviews

Review management IS marketing work.

## MASTER | Execution

### Local Business Process

1. Claim and optimize Google Business Profile completely
2. Create standardized NAP documentation
3. Sync information across Yelp / Facebook / TripAdvisor
4. Actively invite satisfied customers to leave reviews
5. Respond promptly to reviews, especially negative ones
6. Continuously update photos, hours, and descriptions

### Getting Reviews Practically

- Ask at moments when service experience was strong
- Build genuine interaction first, then request review
- Make it clear which platforms matter most
- Focus on quality, not just quantity

### Responding to Negative Reviews

- First acknowledge their feelings
- Then explain improvements or solutions
- Show future customers you take problems seriously

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Is Google Business Profile claimed and fully optimized?
- [ ] Is NAP consistent across all platforms?
- [ ] Do I have a systematic way to gather reviews?
- [ ] Do I respond to reviews promptly?
- [ ] Are my platform profiles regularly maintained?

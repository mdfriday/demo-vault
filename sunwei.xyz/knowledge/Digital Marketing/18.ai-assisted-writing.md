## What is This?

AI-assisted writing isn't completing your marketing thinking for you. It accelerates producing first drafts, variations, structure organization, and polished expression.

## KNOW | Foundation

### Best Uses of AI Writing Tools

Typical applications from the course:
- Generate blog post drafts
- Create social media posts
- Write email drafts
- Compose customer service responses
- Write cold outreach copy
- Generate multiple headline or subject line variations

### AI's Advantages

- Reduces blank-page anxiety
- Increases writing speed
- Enables quick multiple versions
- Bridges gap between "can't write" and "write-then-refine"

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

### What AI Should NOT Replace

It cannot replace:
- Market research
- User understanding
- Value judgment and strategy
- Long-term brand voice development
- Verification of facts, examples, details

The best process is:

**Humans provide direction and judgment. AI provides speed and elaboration.**

### Most Effective Usage Pattern

Let AI produce:
- Outlines
- First drafts
- Multiple headline versions
- CTA variations
- Email subject line options

Then you refine based on customer insights.

## MASTER | Execution

### Recommended Process

1. Define content goal clearly
2. Feed AI: audience, tone, desired action
3. Let it output multiple versions
4. Select the closest version
5. Enhance with real customer language, actual case studies, specific benefits

### Repeatable AI Tasks

- Generate 10 headline alternatives
- Translate product features into customer benefits
- Condense long text into email/social/landing page versions
- Create 5 tone variations of the same CTA

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Do I clarify the goal before AI writes?
- [ ] Do I provide audience and context information?
- [ ] Do I maintain human review and judgment?
- [ ] Do I replace generic phrases with real customer language?
- [ ] Does AI increase speed without decreasing quality?

## What is This?

This is the core research methodology from the course: Use a very short survey to quickly uncover user pain points, desired outcomes, and the market opportunities that haven't been well addressed yet.

## KNOW | Foundation

### Core Principle

The shorter the survey, the higher the response rate. The course recommends using 3 main questions plus an optional email field.

### The Three Core Questions

**Question 1: What's your biggest difficulty with [TOPIC]?**
- This uncovers pain points

**Question 2: If this problem was solved well, what would change in your life/work?**
- This reveals desired outcomes and benefits

**Question 3: How difficult has it been to find a good answer so far?**
- Options: Not difficult / Somewhat difficult / Very difficult
- This identifies market gaps and commercial opportunities

**Optional Question 4:**
- Would you be willing to leave your email if we provide a solution?
- This builds your email list while surveying

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

### Why These Three Questions Are Powerful

**Question 1** finds **pain points** - the specific problems that cause friction  
**Question 2** finds **desire and benefit** - what users want to become  
**Question 3** finds **market gaps** - which problems are unsolved and represent opportunity

If a problem is painful, the outcome matters greatly, AND current solutions are hard to find, that's usually your highest-priority topic.

### This Is NOT a Satisfaction Survey

This isn't asking "what color do you prefer?" or "how happy are you?" Instead, you're targeting:
- High-value problems
- Purchase motivation
- Copy materials
- Potential email subscribers

## MASTER | Execution

### Standard Template

You can use this directly:

1. **What's your biggest difficulty with [TOPIC]?**
2. **If this problem was solved well, what change would it bring to your life, work, or business? Please be specific.**
3. **How difficult has it been to find a good answer so far?**
4. **If we built a better solution, would you like to be notified first via email?**

### Implementation Tips

- Use Google Forms or any simple form tool
- Make the topic specific, not too broad
- Use open-ended questions, avoid forcing specific answers
- Use plain, conversational language that's easy to understand

### Indicators of a High-Quality Survey

- Respondents can complete it in 3-5 minutes
- Questions don't lead or bias answers
- You get quotable original responses
- Results surface "hard to solve" needs
- You build an early email list as a side benefit

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Is the survey short enough?
- [ ] Does it cover pain points, desires, and difficulty level?
- [ ] Does it give respondents space to express themselves?
- [ ] Does it include contact info collection (emails)?
- [ ] Does it focus on one clear topic, not too broad?

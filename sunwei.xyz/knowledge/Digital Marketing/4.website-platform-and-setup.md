# 4. Website Platform & Setup

## What is This?

Your website is the central hub of your marketing system. No matter where traffic comes from—email, search, social, or links—your website is where you capture attention and convert it into relationships.

## KNOW | Foundation

### The Website's Roles

Your website isn't just an "online business card." It carries at least four critical functions:

- Shows who you are and what you do
- Captures user attention  
- Collects emails and leads
- Accepts search traffic and enables conversion

### Why The Course Recommends WordPress

The original course logic:
- High market share—skills transfer across projects
- Open-source and portable—you're not locked into a platform
- Rich plugin and theme ecosystem—highly extensible
- Strong self-control—you retain authority over your asset

### The Difference: WordPress.org vs WordPress.com

- **WordPress.org** - Self-hosted, fully under your control, extensible
- **WordPress.com** - Hosted service, simpler but more restricted

For marketing and growth, the course leans toward self-hosted WordPress.org because you need control for:
- SEO implementation
- Email list building
- Plugin extensions
- Analytics integration
- Continuous optimization

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

### Why Control Matters in Marketing

Marketing isn't a one-time activity. You need to continuously:
- Run SEO experiments
- Collect form data
- Install analytics pixels
- A/B test pages
- Build integrations
- Iterate on design

Closed platforms limit these capabilities. Open platforms allow growth.

### The Real Goal Isn't "Launch," It's "Iterate"

The course emphasizes speed to launch, but the real value is building a website you can modify:
- Change your homepage
- Update buttons
- Modify navigation
- Add new pages
- Add forms
- Add content

The website becomes an evolving marketing asset, not a static brochure.

## MASTER | Execution

### Minimum Viable Website Components

Every site needs at least:
- Homepage
- About page
- Contact/Services page
- Email capture page or form
- Basic navigation menu

### Recommended Setup Sequence

1. Define website purpose: showcase, lead generation, product sales, or content hub?
2. Choose platform and hosting provider
3. Install a clean, lightweight theme
4. Prioritize: finish homepage and navigation
5. Add forms, CTAs, and basic SEO metadata
6. Launch, then iterate—don't pursue perfection before launch

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Is my website purpose clear?
- [ ] Can I modify page content myself?
- [ ] Does navigation clearly show site structure?
- [ ] Is there at least a minimum viable structure to accept traffic?
- [ ] Is the foundation ready for SEO and email integration?

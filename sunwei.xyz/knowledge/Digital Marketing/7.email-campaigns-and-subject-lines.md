## What is This?

This covers the execution side: How to actually send marketing emails, and how to improve open and click rates through subject lines, preview text, CTAs, and data analysis.

## KNOW | Foundation

### Key Components of Effective Marketing Emails

- Recipient list
- Sender name and address
- Subject line
- Preview text
- Email body structure
- Call-to-action (CTA)
- Testing and analysis

### Course Emphasis

- Always use brand domain email as sender address
- Subject lines determine whether emails get opened
- Preview text supplements the subject line
- Always send test emails to yourself before final send
- Track performance metrics after sending

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

### Why Subject Lines Are Critical

If users don't open, the body content is wasted. Subject lines carry the entire responsibility for earning that first click.

### Most Effective Method: Study Your Own Inbox

The course offers practical guidance:
- Open your own email inbox
- Identify emails you actually opened
- Analyze why those subject lines worked
- Also study unopened emails to understand what didn't work

### Common High-Performing Subject Line Characteristics

- **Numbers**: "7 Trends..." or "11 Tactics..."
- **Clear benefit**: "Boost by 600%" or "Save 5 hours weekly"
- **Curiosity gap**: "You won't believe what happened next"
- **Time-sensitive**: New year, limited time, trending topic
- **Personalization**: Name, specific scenario, concrete results

## MASTER | Execution

### Pre-Send Email Process

1. Define one core action you want the reader to take
2. Choose a trustworthy, professional sender identity
3. Write 5-10 subject line variations
4. Use preview text to fill information gaps
5. Keep body focused on one core benefit
6. Create clear, single, clickable CTA
7. Send test email—check typos, formatting, links, mobile rendering
8. Send live, then track: opens, clicks, unsubscribes, site conversions

### Key Performance Metrics to Monitor

- **Open Rate** - How effective is the subject line?
- **Click Rate** - How compelling are the body copy and CTA?
- **Unsubscribe Rate** - Does content match subscriber expectations?
- **Site Behavior** - Do visitors take desired actions after clicking?

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Is the sender identity professional and recognizable?
- [ ] Does the subject line have sufficient pull?
- [ ] Does preview text complement the subject line?
- [ ] Is there only one primary CTA?
- [ ] Did I send and review test emails?
- [ ] Am I tracking open rates, click rates, and unsubscribe rates?

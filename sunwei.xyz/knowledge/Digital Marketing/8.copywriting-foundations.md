## What is This?

Copywriting isn't about "writing beautifully"—it's about **using words to drive action**. The course defines it as: **"Salesmanship in text form"**—persuasion through writing.

## KNOW | Foundation

### The Core Task of Copy

Copy isn't meant to dump information. It's meant to push users toward:
- Clicking
- Subscribing
- Replying
- Leaving contact info
- Purchasing
- Sharing

### Where Copy Shows Up

- Website homepage
- Landing pages
- Emails
- SEO titles and meta descriptions
- Button text
- Ad copy
- Social media posts

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

### Good Copy Isn't About Fancy Words—It's About Understanding Reality

The course's core point:
- First, understand customer pain and desire
- Then, use language they're familiar with
- Finally, clearly tell them what to do next

So the most important prerequisite isn't writing talent—it's customer understanding.

### Copy Really Sells Results, Not Features

Users typically don't want to buy features. They buy:
- Speed
- Convenience
- Savings
- Confidence
- Status
- Ease
- Peace of mind

So remember: **Features describe what the product has. Benefits describe what the user gets.**

## MASTER | Execution

### Three Most Practical Copywriting Tools

1. Before writing anything, ask: "What action do I want this to drive?"
2. Translate product features into user benefits
3. Use customer language as much as possible

### Exercises to Build Copy Skills

- Deconstruct excellent headlines and landing pages
- Rewrite your own website homepage copy
- Create multiple versions of the same message
- Document frequently asked questions and turn them into copy

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Does my copy serve one specific action?
- [ ] Am I describing user benefits, not just features?
- [ ] Am I using language customers would naturally use?
- [ ] Can readers quickly understand the value proposition?
- [ ] Is the copy specific, not generic?

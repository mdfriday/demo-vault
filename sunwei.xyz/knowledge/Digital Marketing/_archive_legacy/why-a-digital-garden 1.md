# Why I Turned My Personal Website into a Digital Garden

> Status: 🌱 Seedling: will keep changing as I practice

## Core idea

Digital Gardens emerged because people began to realize: **you need to own your intellectual property, and let that knowledge keep growing.**

- A blog is a timeline: once written, a post sinks.
- A Digital Garden is a map: notes link to each other and keep being revised and growing over time.

## Why it suits me

My approach is Build in Public: let doing replace talking, and honestly document the journey from employee to independent entrepreneur.

What this process produces isn't pieces of "content"; it's **assets**:

- One user conversation → one Learning
- One Learning → one product decision
- One product decision → one Building Note
- Several Building Notes → one Knowledge note

One real event produces multiple assets. A Digital Garden is exactly the kind of structure that can hold this "growing" process.

## How I do it

- I write in Obsidian; the notes are the source files and belong entirely to me.

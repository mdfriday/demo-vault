# Google Analytics

> This knowledge base organizes Google Analytics into a system covering **setup/configuration, goals/events, traffic reports, segmentation, data quality, and platform integration**.

## Core Philosophy

Google Analytics value isn't just "viewing reports"—it's:

**Install tracking correctly, set goals correctly, then use reports to understand where users come from, what they do, where they drop off, then reverse-engineer that data into content, page, and channel optimization.**

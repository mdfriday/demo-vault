## What is This?

This note summarizes Instagram account fundamentals: transitioning from personal to business account, optimizing avatar, bio, and link.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Business accounts provide more commerce features and analytics
- Avatar, username, bio, and link are the four core homepage elements
- Homepage must quickly explain who you are and who you're for

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Instagram is visual, but users still rely heavily on bio for decisions
- Clearer homepage expression converts browsers into followers and clickers

## MASTER | Execution

- Switch to business account
- Use clear bio that communicates positioning and value
- Keep brand avatar and name consistent

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Have I switched to business account?
- [ ] Does bio clearly communicate positioning?
- [ ] Does link direct to key target page?

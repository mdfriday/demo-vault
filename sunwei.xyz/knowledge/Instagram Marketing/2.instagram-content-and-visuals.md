# 2. Instagram Content & Visual Expression

## What is This?

This note focuses on Instagram's core asset: photos, short videos, visual consistency, and content style.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Instagram is high-visual weight platform
- Content can be original, curated, remixed, or curation-style posts
- Visual style directly impacts brand recognition

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Users are attracted to visuals first, then decide whether to read captions or follow
- Content consistency builds the expectation: "I know what you'll post"

## MASTER | Execution

- Define several fixed content types
- Maintain consistency in cover, color palette, composition, or messaging style
- Use visuals to carry value, not just pursue aesthetics

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Do I have stable visual style?
- [ ] Do I have fixed content pillars?
- [ ] Does each post balance visuals and information?

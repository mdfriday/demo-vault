# 3. Hashtags, Stories & Discovery Mechanisms

## What is This?

This note summarizes two important traffic sources on Instagram: hashtags and Stories.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Hashtags help content enter topic discovery streams
- Stories are better for lightweight, immediate, everyday interaction
- Both carry the responsibility of "being seen by more people"

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Hashtag goal is relevant discovery, not blind volume stacking
- Stories work better for sustained presence and light interaction
- Posts and Stories play different roles: posts archive, Stories accompany

## MASTER | Execution

- Use highly relevant hashtag combinations
- Use Stories for updates, behind-the-scenes, reminders, and light interaction
- Make Stories and main feed content support each other

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Are hashtags focused on content topic, not generic traffic?
- [ ] Are Stories being used consistently?
- [ ] Is there stable discovery and return-visit mechanism?

## What is This?

This note covers two common growth actions on Instagram: boosting natural follows and extending reach through @mentions.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Early growth requires active participation and leverage
- @Mentions can carry content into broader networks
- Follower growth should prioritize relevance over numbers

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Not all exposure equals quality growth
- Being noticed by relevant accounts is more valuable than random traffic
- Mentions should be based on genuine relevance and content context

## MASTER | Execution

- First identify the core audience you want to attract
- Naturally @mention brands, people, or partners in relevant content
- Use content quality to leverage exposure, not just chase visibility

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Are followers increasingly relevant?
- [ ] Are @mentions natural and meaningful?
- [ ] Does homepage have strong enough content to convert visibility?

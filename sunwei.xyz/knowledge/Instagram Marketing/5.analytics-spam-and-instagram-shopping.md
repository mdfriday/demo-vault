# 5. Analytics, Spam Management & Instagram Shopping

## What is This?

This note summarizes three later-stage operations: reading data, managing spam, and e-commerce features.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Analytics help you assess content and audience performance
- Spam reduces interaction quality and account experience
- Shopping features connect content more directly to products

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Analytics focus is finding high-performing content and valuable audience
- Cleaning spam makes interactions authentic and judgment more reliable
- Shopping works best when pages, content, and product paths are clear

## MASTER | Execution

- Regularly review engagement and audience changes
- Remove obvious spam accounts and low-value interactions
- If selling products, design natural "content→product" transitions

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Do I regularly check Instagram analytics?
- [ ] Am I addressing obvious spam issues?
- [ ] Am I clear on Instagram's role in the sales path?

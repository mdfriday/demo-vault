## What is This?

This note summarizes LinkedIn foundations: establishing your professional identity, domain, and value proposition clearly.

## KNOW | Foundation

- LinkedIn is a professional and business relationship platform
- Profile carries trust-building and professional expression functions
- Title, summary, experience, and avatar are all critical

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Users typically judge from profile whether you're worth connecting with or collaborating with
- Clearer positioning attracts higher-quality connections

## MASTER | Execution

- Define professional direction in one clear statement
- Highlight achievements, experience, and value you provide
- Keep profile consistent with content direction

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Does title clearly communicate professional positioning?
- [ ] Does profile demonstrate credibility and achievements?
- [ ] Is avatar professionally consistent with personal brand?

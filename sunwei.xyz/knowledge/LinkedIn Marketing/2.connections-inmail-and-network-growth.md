## What is This?

This note summarizes core LinkedIn growth actions: building relevant connections, initiating conversations, expanding high-quality professional networks.

## KNOW | Foundation

- LinkedIn growth is fundamentally relationship growth
- Connection requests, messaging, and follow-up interactions all matter
- Quality connections have more long-term value than generic connections

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- LinkedIn is not purely a follower platform—it's a professional relationship platform
- Each connection can impact future visibility, conversations, or business opportunities

## MASTER | Execution

- Prioritize connecting with relevant industry, customers, partners, potential allies
- Keep messaging short, clear, natural
- Treat connections as long-term network assets

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Are connections aligned with target direction?
- [ ] Are messages natural, not templated sales pitches?
- [ ] Is there consistent relationship maintenance?

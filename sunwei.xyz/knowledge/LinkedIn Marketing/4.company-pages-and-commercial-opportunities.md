## What is This?

This note summarizes LinkedIn's brand pages and business applications: not just company showcases, but also serving recruitment, brand endorsement, partnership, and ad preparation.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Personal and company identity on LinkedIn can reinforce each other
- Company pages enable brand presence, team display, and organizational reach
- Platform extends to advertising and formal business engagement

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Many business opportunities don't come directly from content but from "looking professionally credible"
- Company pages are tools for organizing individual influence at organizational level

## MASTER | Execution

- Create company pages when you need organizational endorsement
- Make company pages interconnect with personal profile, content, and website
- View LinkedIn as B2B relationships and credibility platform

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Do I need company pages for organizational support?
- [ ] Do company page and personal brand mutually reinforce?
- [ ] Am I leveraging LinkedIn's business credibility attributes?

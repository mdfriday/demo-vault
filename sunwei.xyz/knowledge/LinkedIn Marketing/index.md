# LinkedIn Marketing

> This knowledge base organizes LinkedIn Marketing into a system covering **profile optimization, connection growth, content distribution, groups, company pages, and commercial opportunities**.

## Core Philosophy

LinkedIn marketing starts by establishing clear professional identity, then builds credibility through connections, content, and industry participation, finally converting professional attention into relationships, traffic, or business opportunities.

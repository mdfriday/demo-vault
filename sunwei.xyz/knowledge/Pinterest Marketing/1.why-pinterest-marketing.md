# 1. Why Pinterest Marketing?

## What is This?

This note explains Pinterest's unique value: it's more like a long-term visual search and curation platform, not a pure instant-social platform.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Pinterest users enter with intent to find inspiration, solutions, resources
- Content can be saved, rediscovered, and shared over long periods
- Excellent for website traffic and content asset repurposing

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Pinterest content usually has longer lifespan than many instant feeds
- Ideal for topics with clear visual presentation and collection value

## MASTER | Execution

- Treat Pinterest as visual SEO channel
- Prioritize publishing content worth saving and searching for

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Is my content suitable for visual presentation?
- [ ] Is it worth saving and rediscovering?
- [ ] Can it drive long-term website traffic?

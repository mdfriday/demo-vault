# 3. Profile Structure, Boards & Content Organization

## What is This?

This note summarizes Pinterest's structured operations: not just posting images, but organizing topics with boards so users and platform understand your content system.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Boards are Pinterest's topic organization unit
- Profile structure impacts user browsing paths and platform understanding
- Clear categorization helps content be discovered and saved

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Pinterest is more like content directory than instant feed
- Board naming and topic clarity directly impact long-term discovery

## MASTER | Execution

- Create boards based on audience-interest topics
- Name them clearly, specifically, searchably
- Make boards well-organized yet interconnected

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Are boards clearly categorized by topic?
- [ ] Are names easy to understand and searchable?
- [ ] Does profile structure help users continue browsing?

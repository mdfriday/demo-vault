# Pinterest Marketing

> This knowledge base organizes Pinterest Marketing into a system covering **account setup, website verification, board structure, pin design, follower growth, and tool usage**.

## Core Philosophy

Pinterest marketing treats it as a "visual search engine" rather than a typical social platform: first integrate account and website, then use structured boards and quality pins to consistently acquire search-based traffic.

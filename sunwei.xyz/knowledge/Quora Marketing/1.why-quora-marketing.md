# 1. Why Quora Marketing?

## What is This?

This note explains Quora's core value: why it's suitable as a platform for content marketing, brand building, and targeted traffic.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Quora is question-and-answer based
- Platform users enter with specific questions
- Great for establishing expert status and long-term search traffic

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Unlike feed platforms, Quora attention typically comes from "actively seeking answers"
- Such users have stronger intent and are closer to conversion
- One quality answer can drive long-term exposure and clicks

## MASTER | Execution

- View Quora as "question-capture platform"
- Prioritize answering questions highly relevant to your business
- Use answers to build authority, not just one-time traffic

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Do I understand Quora's high-intent characteristic?
- [ ] Do I view it as long-term content asset?
- [ ] Do I prioritize questions highly relevant to business?

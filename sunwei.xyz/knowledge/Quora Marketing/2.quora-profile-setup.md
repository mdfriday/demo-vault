# 2. Quora Account Setup & Optimization

## What is This?

This note summarizes the role of Quora profile: not static business card, but key page determining whether users trust you after reading your answer.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Quora profile impacts answer credibility
- Users often click into author profile after reading
- Profile must explain: who you are, why you're worth listening to, where to learn more

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- On Quora, identity credibility directly impacts answer persuasiveness
- A clear, professional, relevant profile increases likelihood of clicking personal homepage and off-site links

## MASTER | Execution

- Clearly state your domain background and professional tags
- Keep profile aligned with business direction
- Add appropriate off-site links or brand entry points

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Does bio reflect professional relevance?
- [ ] Does it clearly state what I'm expert in?
- [ ] Are there appropriate follow-up entry points?

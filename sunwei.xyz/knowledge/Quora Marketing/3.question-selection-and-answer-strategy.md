## What is This?

This note covers Quora marketing's core execution: choosing right questions and providing answers worth reading, upvoting, saving, and clicking.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Good questions matter more than writing skill
- Answer structure determines if users finish reading
- Question selection balances relevance, popularity, and conversion potential

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Answering isn't essay-writing—it's targeting question intent
- Good answers typically have clear structure, specificity, examples
- Better question selection means answers continue delivering value

## MASTER | Execution

- Prioritize questions most relevant to your business
- Use simple structure: direct answer, then detail, then resources
- Build library of recurring question answers

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Is question relevant to target audience?
- [ ] Does answer lead with direct response?
- [ ] Do I use examples to strengthen credibility?
- [ ] Can this become long-term content asset?

# 4. Soft Promotion & Quora Business Pages

## What is This?

This note summarizes promoting on Quora without appearing pushy: use helpful content as front stage, natural resource guidance as back stage.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Hard advertising doesn't work on Quora
- Effective method: high-quality answers + natural resource guidance
- Business Pages help brand presentation of content and identity

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Users come to Quora to solve problems, not to see ads
- If your link naturally extends the answer, click intent is higher
- Soft promotion's prerequisite is providing truly useful answers first

## MASTER | Execution

- Only add resources where necessary in answers
- Links should serve the question, not "what I want to promote"
- Use Business Pages for brand presentation when needed

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Have I avoided hard selling?
- [ ] Do links truly help users continue problem-solving?
- [ ] Does Business Page serve brand hosting, not just exist?

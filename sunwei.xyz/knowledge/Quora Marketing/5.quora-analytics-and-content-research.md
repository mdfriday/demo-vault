## What is This?

This note summarizes Quora's additional value in content research: not just traffic generation, but helping discover what users really ask.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Quora questions themselves are user intent database
- Analytics help identify which answers get most exposure and engagement
- Quora can also inspire blogs, videos, and email content

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- User question phrasing often feels more natural than marketer-created headlines
- High-performing answers often reveal truly valuable topics worth expanding
- So Quora is both channel and research tool

## MASTER | Execution

- Regularly organize frequent questions
- Upgrade high-performing answers into longer content
- Use question language to inform website titles, SEO, and social content

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Do I view Quora as content research source?
- [ ] Am I tracking which answers work best?
- [ ] Do I apply high-frequency questions to other channels?

## What is This?

This note summarizes what to post on Twitter and how to maintain presence and value with short content.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Twitter content is short, fast, frequent, and discussable
- Common content: opinions, tips, links, updates, questions, trending responses
- What you post determines why people follow you

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Twitter isn't for long explanation—it's for "continuous presence"
- Same theme can be split into many tweets over time
- Content is both output and participation in public discussion

## MASTER | Execution

- Build around several fixed themes
- Balance: opinions, value, links, and engagement questions
- Use threads, replies, and retweets to extend content

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Do I have fixed topic range?
- [ ] Is content concise and clear?
- [ ] Do I have both output and engagement content?

# 3. Followers, Hashtags & Growth

## What is This?

This note covers three critical things in Twitter cold-start growth: follow relationships, hashtags, and initial visibility.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Early follower growth depends on active participation, not just tweeting
- Hashtags help content enter broader topic streams
- Following right accounts and joining discussions matters more than chasing numbers

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Hashtag value is topic categorization, not stuffing
- Following relevant accounts and participating in their conversations is how you enter circles
- Growth key is getting relevant people to see you, not random people

## MASTER | Execution

- Use small, high-relevant hashtags
- Participate in industry keyword discussions
- Deliberately build connections with peers, potential customers, media accounts

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Are hashtags few and relevant?
- [ ] Am I participating in target circle conversations?
- [ ] Is follower growth leaning toward relevant audience vs. random?

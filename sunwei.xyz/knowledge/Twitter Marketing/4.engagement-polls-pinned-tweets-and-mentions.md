# 4. Engagement, Polls, Pinned Tweets & @Mentions

## What is This?

This note covers high-leverage Twitter engagement tools: polls, pinned tweets, @mentions, and conversation participation.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Polls lower engagement barriers quickly
- Pinned tweet determines what new visitors see first
- @Mentions can bring content into specific personal networks

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Twitter spread often comes from conversation, not just standalone posts
- Pinned tweet is "homepage CTA"—good for important content
- @Mentions should connect and reference, not spam

## MASTER | Execution

- Use polls to gather opinions and create light engagement
- Pin your most value-representative tweet
- Use @Mentions in relevant contexts to increase visibility and relevance

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Have I used polls to lower engagement barriers?
- [ ] Does pinned tweet serve clear goal?
- [ ] Are @Mentions contextual and necessary?

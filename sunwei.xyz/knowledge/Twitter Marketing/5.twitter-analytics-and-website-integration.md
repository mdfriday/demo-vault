# 5. Twitter Analytics & Website Integration

## What is This?

This note covers measuring Twitter content performance and ensuring Twitter serves larger business goals, not just staying within the platform.

## KNOW | Foundation

- Twitter Analytics shows impressions, engagement, clicks performance
- Twitter marketing ultimately serves bigger business goals: website visits, subscriptions, product awareness, customer relationships
- Need bridges between platform content and off-site assets

## UNDERSTAND | Deep Dive

- Not every tweet needs to drive traffic, but overall should have direction
- Analytics value is finding: what content gets seen, engaged with, and clicked
- Without website or landing page integration, platform attention doesn't stick

## MASTER | Execution

- Regularly review high-performing tweets
- Observe which content better drives traffic vs. builds influence
- Create clear website entry points in bio, pinned tweets, and content

### Knowledge Checklist

- [ ] Do I regularly check Twitter Analytics?
- [ ] Do I know which tweets drive most clicks?
- [ ] Am I directing traffic to key pages, not random links?

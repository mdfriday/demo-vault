> This knowledge base organizes Twitter Marketing into a system covering **account optimization, content strategy, follower growth, engagement tactics, and website integration**.

## Core Philosophy

Twitter marketing isn't constant tweeting—it's:

**Make account clear and credible, then use frequent short content and topic participation to enter feeds, expand visibility through engagement and hashtags, and guide attention to website, products, or relationships.**

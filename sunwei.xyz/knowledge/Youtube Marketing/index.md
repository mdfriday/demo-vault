# YouTube Marketing

> This knowledge base organizes YouTube Marketing into a system covering **channel setup, content production, video SEO, thumbnails/metadata optimization, analytics, and monetization**.

## Core Philosophy

YouTube marketing isn't mechanically posting videos—it's:

**Build channel and content positioning first, then consistently create videos that get clicked, get searched for, and retain watch time, finally optimize topics and packaging with data.**

## Overall Flow

```
01 Clarify why YouTube
    ↓
02 Create & optimize channel
    ↓
03 Find topics & keywords
    ↓
04 Record & edit first videos
    ↓
05 Optimize titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails
    ↓
06 Use cards, end screens, banners for retention
    ↓
07 Use Analytics to find high-performing content
    ↓
08 Then consider monetization & scaling
```

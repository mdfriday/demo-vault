## Try MDFriday Publish

Install **MDFriday Publish** from Obsidian's Community plugins (<https://obsidian.md/plugins?search=mdfriday-publish>), right-click a note, and publish. Guest mode needs no account. Guest content is cleared at the next UTC midnight, and Free plan content is cleared on the 1st of each month, so both are for trying things out. Personal (about \$5–6/month, 1 GB) keeps your site permanently.

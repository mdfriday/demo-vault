# Blogging Features

← [[mdfriday/voice-of-custom/index|Voice of the Customer]]

## What users are saying

People who want to use Obsidian Publish or a digital garden as a blog get stuck on a few things: no comment section (some call it the only blocker), an RSS feed with no dates, reversed order and no settings, no "recent posts" on the home page, no excerpts or cover images, and no newsletter or way to monetize.

> [!info] Signal (approximate)
> ~23 VOC cards · ~9 YouTube comments (4 on comments, 1 on RSS, 2 on monetization, etc.).
> Ranked **#11**: bloggers treat it as a blocker.

## Why it hurts

- **A blog is a conversation, not a one-way broadcast.** Without comments or subscriptions, writers get no feedback and can't keep readers.
- **Digital gardens and blogs are read differently.** A garden is explored through connections; a blog is read in time order. Supporting only one loses the other half of users.
- **Monetization is where many knowledge creators want to end up.** This ties directly to MDFriday's vision, "Help people turn knowledge into businesses".

## How people cope today

- Moving from Publish to platforms with full blogging features, such as Ghost or WordPress.
- Wiring up a third-party comment system, or assembling RSS and list pages in Hugo.
- Running subscriptions separately on a newsletter platform, and then running into the [[sync-and-update-workflow|copy-paste workflow]].

## MDFriday's response

- **Multiple themes**: the theme options can include blog-style themes (specific layouts unconfirmed).
- RSS (with dates, newest first), recent-posts lists, comments, subscriptions / monetization: **all (unconfirmed)**.

# Payment & Renewal

← [[mdfriday/voice-of-custom/index|Voice of the Customer]]

## What users are saying

If the payment method doesn't fit, the sale doesn't happen, and neither does the renewal. Chinese users who paid with WeChat can't find a way to renew when their plan expires; some people don't know how to buy a subscription with PayPal; privacy-minded users don't want to give their credit card details to any platform and want crypto support; some government employees aren't allowed to keep a saved card on an account.

> [!info] Signal (approximate)
> ~4 web supplement sources (Chinese and English); latest evidence 2025-06.
> Relevance to MDFriday: **medium**, directly affecting whether Chinese users can pay and whether they renew.

## Why it hurts

- **Payment is the last step of the conversion funnel.** The user has already decided to buy and is blocked by the payment method: the most frustrating kind of churn.
- **Renewals are easier to lose than first payments.** At first purchase people are motivated to find the way; at expiry, if they can't find it, they quietly drop off.
- **Payment habits differ widely between groups.** Chinese users are used to WeChat Pay / Alipay, Western users to credit cards / PayPal, and some have compliance or privacy constraints.

## How people cope today

- Posting to ask how to renew and waiting for the company or a helpful user to reply.
- Using someone else's card or switching payment method.
- Giving up on renewing.

## MDFriday's response

- What's known today is the three plans (Guest / Free / Personal); which payment methods are supported is **(unconfirmed)**.
- Suggestions: support Alipay / WeChat Pay for Chinese users; remind users before expiry and give a grace period after it; offer a one-time annual payment without saving a card (**all unconfirmed**).
- The grace period needs to be designed together with the [[vendor-trust-and-reliability|clearing rules]]: when a paid plan expires, does the site go offline immediately, enter a grace period, or drop to Free and get cleared under Free rules?

## Open questions

- How do existing Chinese customers pay? Has anyone churned because of the payment method?
- Are the rules for what happens to a site after Personal expires written down clearly and communicated to users?

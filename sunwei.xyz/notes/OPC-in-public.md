This note is still evolving. It captures my current thinking about who I am, what I'm building, and how I want to share the journey.

## My Journey

I'm a programmer who loves technology and wants to build my own business with the help of AI.

I believe technology and AI can help me build assets that create lasting value. Instead of always trading my time for income, I want to gradually build a business where the assets I create can generate value over time.

This is not just a business experiment. It's also a personal journey: moving from being an employee to becoming an independent entrepreneur, learning by doing, and sharing what I discover along the way.

## Who I Am

**Technologist + Builder + Entrepreneur**

- **Technologist:** I love understanding how things work and using technology to solve problems.
- **Builder:** I turn ideas into real products, experiment with different approaches, and learn from what works and what doesn't.
- **Entrepreneur:** I want to turn what I know and build into something valuable to others, and create a sustainable business around it.

I'm not trying to present myself as someone who has already figured everything out. I'm documenting the process of figuring it out.

## My Guiding Principles

> Own your knowledge.  
> Build your business.  
> Enjoy your life.

I believe we should own our knowledge, create things that belong to us, and use technology to build a life with more freedom.

Knowledge should be more than something we consume or store. It can grow into ideas, products, services, and businesses that create value for others.

That's one reason I believe in Digital Gardens: knowledge is not a finished collection of articles, but something that grows, connects, and evolves over time. Our websites can become places where we own our intellectual property and develop it into something meaningful.

## Building in Public

I want to share my transformation from employee to independent entrepreneur honestly and openly.

That means sharing not only finished products and successful outcomes, but also the decisions, experiments, mistakes, frustrations, and unexpected discoveries along the way.

Rather than telling people what they should do, I want to show what I'm doing, explain why I'm doing it, and share what I learn from the experience.

My personal website will become a Digital Garden that documents how my knowledge grows alongside my business.

## My Platforms

Each platform has a different role in this journey.

### sunwei.xyz — Knowledge and the Journey

This is my personal Digital Garden and the home of my thinking.

I'll document what I'm learning, the ideas I'm exploring, the decisions I'm making, and the process of building a one-person business.

Think of it as a documentary of my journey: an evolving record of how knowledge turns into action, and how action creates new knowledge.

### mdfriday.com — Products and Business

MDFriday is where I turn ideas into products and help other people do the same.

Its vision is:

> Help people turn knowledge into businesses.

Its product positioning is:

> Build websites that help knowledge creators grow, sell, and serve.

I want to help people turn the Markdown knowledge they already own into websites, and gradually turn those websites into meaningful businesses.

MDFriday is not separate from my personal journey. It's one of the main things I'm building, and a real-world example of how technology, knowledge, and AI can work together to create value.

### YouTube — Stories and Experiences

YouTube is where I share the human side of the journey.

I'll talk about the emotional ups and downs of building a business independently, the challenges I encounter, the things that surprise me, and the interesting experiences along the way.

I'll also share product demos and the process of building MDFriday. These aren't separate from my personal story; they're part of it.

I want people to see not just what I build, but what it's actually like to build it.

## My Core Idea

**Let doing replace talking.**

I don't need to have every answer before I begin, nor do I need to establish myself as an expert before sharing anything.

I'll build, experiment, reflect, and share what I learn. Over time, people can get to know me through my work, my decisions, and the things I create.

I want to stay consistent and authentic, letting the work speak for itself.

This website, MDFriday, and my YouTube channel are all parts of the same journey: using technology and AI to build assets, turning knowledge into a business, and creating a life on my own terms.

I'm not just talking about the destination. I'm building my way there, in public.

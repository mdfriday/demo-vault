The topic is not a keyword list and not the writer's story. It is the gap this piece closes for the reader you named.

## How to find it

1. Write their situation in one sentence. If you have their words, use those words.
2. Write the outcome they want in one sentence. It must be something the action in [[01-job|the job]] can move them toward.
3. The topic is the gap between those two sentences: the problem, the mistake, or the method this piece will cover.
4. Add the angle in a few words: how to do it, a mistake to stop, or the outcome to expect. One angle only.
5. Drop the topic if none of the facts can support it. Ask for a narrower topic or for the missing fact. Do not fill the gap with a general essay.

## Shape

`[reader] + [problem or goal] + [one angle]`

A topic about the company ("why we built this") is allowed only when the piece type is a story and the action is still the one you named. Otherwise rewrite it as their problem.

## Reject

- Two topics in one piece. Split them or pick the one tied to the action.
- A topic whose proof would have to be invented ("grow 437%"). Specificity is a later rule, not a license to make up the result.
- A topic you cannot say back in their words when their words were provided.

Write the topic as one sentence. Then go to [[03-headline|the headline]].

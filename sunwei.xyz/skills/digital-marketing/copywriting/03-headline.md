The headline decides whether anyone reads on. Do this step before [[05-body|the body]]. A headline is a promise. If the body cannot keep it, the headline is wrong, not the body.

For an email, this step is the subject line. For a product or sales page, it is the first headline, not a section title.

## Write ten

Ten variations of the topic. Twenty-five only when the user asked to practice headlines. Do not stop at the first line you like.

Each variation uses one of these shapes. Change the words to this topic. Do not copy an example from the knowledge note.

1. Number — "7 ways to …" only if the body will contain that many.
2. How to — "How to [outcome] without [the obstacle they have]".
3. Mistake — "The mistake that …" only if you will name the mistake.
4. Question — a question they would actually ask.
5. Keyword first — "[their phrase]: [the specific outcome]".
6. Specific — outcome plus a time or a concrete object, and only if that number or time is in the facts.
7. Who / what / when — one of those, not all of them stacked.

## Pick one

Prefer, in order:

1. Their words, when you have them.
2. A benefit they would notice, not a feature of the product.
3. One idea. A colon is allowed. A second promise is not.
4. For an article or a search title, about 50–60 characters. Say so if you break that on purpose. An email subject may be a little longer if it is still one idea.

Mark the pick and name its shape.

## Attention, without fake pressure

You may use a curiosity gap, a benefit, or a real constraint (a date you were given). Do not add urgency, a secret, or a rank ("#1", "99% of marketers") unless that fact was given.

## Power words

Words like get, master, now, proven, secret, exclusive are optional. They do not rescue a vague line. "Proven" and "guaranteed" require a fact. If you do not have it, do not use the word.

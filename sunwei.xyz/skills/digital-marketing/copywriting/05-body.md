Fill the skeleton. Do not change its order while you write.

## Benefits, not features

A feature is what it is. A benefit is what changes for them.

For every feature you mention, walk this chain and keep the last step you can support:

1. What it is.
2. What it does for them.
3. The outcome they would notice (time, money, a job, a finished task).

If you cannot reach step 3 from the facts, stop at step 2. Do not invent the dream outcome. "100 hours of video" is not yet a benefit. "You can study in the gaps of a work day" is, if that is true.

Ask "so what?" once. If the answer is still about the product, ask again. Stop when you hit their life, or when you run out of facts.

## Specific, but only with real details

Prefer a concrete object, step, or time you were given over a vague verb ("save time", "get results").

Use a number only when it is in the facts. "47%" from the knowledge note is an illustration of specificity. It is not a result you may attach to this piece.

If you have no number, be specific in kind: the step, the tool, the constraint. Do not write "many people" and do not replace it with a made-up count.

## Emotion

Use emotion to connect a true offer to a real problem. Do not use it to push something the facts do not support.

Allowed, when the underlying fact is true:

- A real cost of doing nothing.
- Curiosity: withhold a detail the body then gives.
- A picture of the outcome you can actually support.
- Belonging: a real group or a real count you were given.
- Urgency: a real date or a real limit.

Not allowed: fake scarcity, a countdown, "your competitors already know", or a fear you cannot tie to the facts.

## Voice

- Write to the one reader. "You" more than "we" or "I". First person only inside a story you were given.
- Short sentences. Contractions. Active voice ("you get", not "it will be gained").
- Plain words. No internal names they would not say.
- One idea per sentence. Bullets when a section is a list. A subheading on every section.
- Cut any word that does not change the meaning.

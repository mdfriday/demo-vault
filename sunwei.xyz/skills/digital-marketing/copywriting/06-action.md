The piece fails if the reader cannot tell what to do next.

## The line

- Start with a verb.
- Say what happens next, in their terms (open an account, read the note, reply).
- Match the action from [[01-job|the job]]. Do not upgrade a "read this" into a purchase.
- One primary action. A sales page may repeat that same action. It may not add a second one.

## Friction

State a lower barrier only if it was given: how long it takes, no account, no card, a refund window. "Takes 2 minutes. No credit card." is allowed only when both are true.

If the first step is large, name the smallest true first step instead of pretending the whole job is easy.

## Risk

If a guarantee, a price, or a way to undo the action was given, say it next to the action. If it was not given, do not invent a money-back line. Honesty about a limit is part of the ask.

## Urgency

Add a deadline or a cap only when you were given the date or the number. Otherwise leave urgency out. A missing countdown is correct. A fake one is a failed draft.

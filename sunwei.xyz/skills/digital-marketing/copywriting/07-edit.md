Read the draft once as the reader. Fail the piece on any unchecked line. Fix it, then return the draft and the log from [[SKILL|Copywriting]].

## Checklist

- [ ] One reader, one action, one piece type
- [ ] The topic is their gap, not the writer's story, unless a story was the assigned piece
- [ ] At least ten headline variations, one pick, and the body keeps that promise
- [ ] Article or search title is about 50–60 characters, or the exception is in the log
- [ ] The skeleton matches the piece type. No extra sections
- [ ] Each feature that appears has an outcome, or it was cut
- [ ] Every number, quote, name, guarantee, and scarcity line is in the facts
- [ ] No example from the knowledge note reused as this product's result
- [ ] "You" carries the piece. Jargon they would not say is gone
- [ ] One primary action, a verb, and a true description of what happens next
- [ ] Nothing asks you to publish or send it

## Common failures

- The headline is the product category ("Email Marketing Guide").
- The first paragraph defines the subject instead of their situation.
- The middle is a feature list.
- Two asks.
- A specific percent with no source.
- The ad or headline promises one thing and the page delivers another.

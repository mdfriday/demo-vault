# Copywriting

Load [[index|the outline]] and follow it in order. Open a step file only when you reach that step.

Copywriting is salesmanship in text. The job is one reader, one action, one piece. Knowledge notes explain the ideas. This folder is the procedure. Later writing notes in Knowledge revise a step file here. They do not each become a new skill.

## When to use

- Write or rewrite an article, email, product description, or sales page.
- The draft has a weak headline, a feature list, or no clear next step.

## When not to use

- The task is to find what people said. That is research, not this skill.
- The task is only a title batch with evidence and view counts. Use the headline swipe file if that skill is in the vault.
- Do not publish. Return a draft.

## Before you write

You need three things. If any is missing, ask. Do not guess a product, a price, or a result.

1. The one reader, as a situation (what they are trying to do).
2. The one action this piece asks for.
3. The facts you may claim: what the thing does, any real number, any real quote, any real limit.

## Order

1. [[01-job|The job]] — reader, action, piece type.
2. [[02-topic|The topic]] — the gap this piece closes.
3. [[03-headline|The headline]] — write this before the body.
4. [[04-structure|The structure]] — the skeleton for this piece type.
5. [[05-body|The body]] — benefits, specificity, emotion, voice.
6. [[06-action|The action]] — the ask, and only honest urgency.
7. [[07-edit|The edit]] — pass or fail before you return the draft.

## Return

- The draft.
- A short log: reader, action, topic, the headline variations, the one you picked and why, the structure you used.
- A list of claims you left out because they were not in the facts.

Numbers in the source lesson (percentages, revenue, student counts) are examples of how specificity sounds. They are not facts about this reader or this product.

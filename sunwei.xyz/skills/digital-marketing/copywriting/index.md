## The job

Write one piece that moves one reader to one action. The default piece is an article. The same order covers an email, a product description, or a sales page. The structure changes. The order does not.

## Order

1. [[01-job|The job]] — name the reader, the action, and the piece type. Stop if these are missing.
2. [[02-topic|The topic]] — state the gap between their situation and the outcome, in their words when you have them.
3. [[03-headline|The headline]] — write the variations and pick one before any body sentence.
4. [[04-structure|The structure]] — fill the skeleton for this piece type. Do not invent a new outline.
5. [[05-body|The body]] — turn features into outcomes, stay specific, use emotion only on a true offer.
6. [[06-action|The action]] — one next step, with friction and risk stated only as given.
7. [[07-edit|The edit]] — run the checklist. Fix failures. Then return the draft.

## Done

- A stranger can say who it is for, what to do, and what they get.
- The headline's promise is kept by the body.
- Every number, quote, and scarcity line comes from the facts you were given.
- There is one primary action.

## What this skill will not do

- Invent testimonials, percentages, revenue, or "only 50 seats".
- Reuse example lines from the knowledge note as if they were this product's results.
- Publish, send, or post.

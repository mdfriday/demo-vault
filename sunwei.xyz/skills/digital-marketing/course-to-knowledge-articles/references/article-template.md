# Knowledge article template

```markdown
---
title: <Idea name>
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags:
  - knowledge
  - <course-topic>
  - <sub-topic>
description: <One sentence: what the idea lets you do.>
---

# <Idea name>

## What it is
Definition in plain words, then the course's key points (numbered if the course lists them). Attribute short quotes.

## Why it matters
What goes wrong without it; when to reach for it.

## How to use it
**Inputs:** ... **Output:** ...
1. Step (link sibling ideas where a step depends on them: [[sibling-article|Sibling]]).
2. ...

> [!example] Illustration: <your context>
> Short, concrete example. Illustrative only; all claims must match the real product.

## How to verify it worked
| Signal | How to measure |
|---|---|
**Good:** ... **Bad:** ...

## How to keep optimizing
- One change at a time; what to test next.

## Common mistakes
- ...

## Checklist
- [ ] (about six concrete, checkable items)

## Related
- [[sibling-article|Sibling Title]]

## Source
<Course>, lecture N "<title>". Which parts are general practice rather than course content.
```

Section guidance:

- **How to use it** must be executable: inputs, output, numbered steps.
- **How to verify** names observable signals (open rate, scroll depth, replies, conversions) and what good vs bad looks like. If the course gives no benchmarks, say so and compare versions against each other.
- **Checklist** items are pass/fail, not advice.

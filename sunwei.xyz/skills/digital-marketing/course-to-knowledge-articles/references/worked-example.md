## Excerpt: The AIDA Formula

> **How to use it.** **Inputs:** your audience's pain points and desires (ideally in their words), the product's key benefits, any proof, and the single action you want. **Output:** a draft where each section does one AIDA job.
> 1. **Decide the action first.**

> 6. **Review stage by stage.** Read only the headline: would you open it? Read only the first paragraph: would you keep reading?

> **How to verify it worked.** Attention → open or click rate; Interest → scroll depth, retention past the intro; Desire → clicks on pricing, replies with questions; Action → sign-ups or installs per visitor. "A big drop at one stage tells you which section to rewrite."

> **Checklist.** One action decided before writing · Headline answers "what's in it for me?" · First paragraph offers something fresh · Desire section has benefits, life change and real proof · Clear, visible CTA with honest incentive or urgency · Each stage measured, weakest stage fixed first

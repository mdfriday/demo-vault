# Headline pattern catalog

## Course patterns (magnetic headlines)

| Pattern | Shape | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Number | "7 Ways to …", "5 Mistakes …" | Sets a concrete, scannable promise |
| How to + even if / without | "How to X Without Y" | Removes the objection that stops people |
| Mistakes | "X Mistakes That …" | Loss aversion; the reader checks themselves |
| Question | "Is X Worth It?" | Mirrors the question the reader is already asking |
| Ultra specific | "… in 5 Minutes, No Terminal" | Specific claims are more believable |
| Five Ws | who/what/when/where/why framing | Answers intent directly |
| Image / visual | a vivid, concrete picture | Memorable; stands out in a feed |

Search-friendly rules: put the keyword first, aim for 50–60 characters so results pages don't truncate it, and use punctuation (colon, parentheses, question mark) to split keyword and hook.

Practice rule: write 10–25 variations, keep a swipe file tagged by pattern with "why it works", and model structure, not wording.

## Evidence-ranked patterns (reference run: Obsidian publishing niche)

Ranked by how much evidence each pattern had across the top 50 YouTube videos, 567 community titles and 18 search queries. Re-rank for your topic.

1. **"[Keyword] for Free" / free alternative.** Top tutorials had 59k–66k views. Honest use only.
2. **"<Incumbent> Alternative(s)".** Appears in search queries and 33 community titles.
3. **Topic noun + setup/benefit** ("Digital Garden"): 19 of 50 videos, 1.24M views, but weak buying intent.
4. **How to + without [objection]** ("without Git", "no terminal"). Mirrors the #1 pain theme.
5. **Turn X into Y** ("Turn Your Notes Into A Website").
6. **First-person story** ("How I…", "Why I switched"): 13 titles, 1.13M views. Use the founder's real voice.
7. **Easiest / share one** ("Easiest way to share a note").
8. **Question** ("What Is …?", "Is … Worth It?").
9. **Head-to-head** ("A vs B vs C: Which Fits?").
10. **Mistakes / what I wish I knew.**

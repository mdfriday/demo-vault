# Worked example: MDFriday blog titles (2026-09)

Inputs: top 50 YouTube videos for "Obsidian publish / digital garden" (views as of 2026-09-20), 18 search queries, 567 VOC card titles.

## Pattern stats (excerpt)

| Pattern in title | Videos | Total views | Median views |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| "digital garden" | 19 | 1,238,589 | 16,098 |
| first person "I / my" | 13 | 1,134,400 | 7,455 |
| "publish" | 19 | 305,587 | 7,188 |
| ends with "?" or "!" | 8 | 232,600 | 17,248 |
| "free" | 8 | 209,934 | 17,986 |

Takeaway: "digital garden" is the traffic layer (lifestyle, huge views, weak install intent); "publish … for free / alternative / Quartz" is the conversion layer (15k–66k views, comments full of setup pain).

Why the picks won: the target keyword comes first (the number-first variant lost to the keyword-first one), the length fits, and the "free" title turns the honesty issue (free tiers are temporary) into the hook instead of hiding it.

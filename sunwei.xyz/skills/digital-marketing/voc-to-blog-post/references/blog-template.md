# Blog post template

```markdown
---
title: "<Keyword-first title, 50–60 chars>"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags:
  - blog
  - <topic>
description: "<140–160 chars: keyword early, the problem, what the post gives.>"
---

## Your real options, honestly compared

**N. <Your product> (the one I built).** More below.

## Why I built <Product> this way

- **<Confirmed feature>.** So you <benefit in the reader's terms>.
```

# Voice of the Customer

> Don't ask "What should I build next?"
> Ask "What are people already struggling with?"

<What this section is: the whole market's voice, not only your users'. The North Star question it serves.>

## What this research is

| Source | Scale (approx.) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VOC cards | N | themes, date span, platform split |
| YouTube comments | M | from X videos |
| Web supplement | K archived pages | sites; quotes verified; distinct URLs |
| Customer list | — | aggregate only (e.g. email-domain split); no personal data |

**Method**
1. How cards and comments were tagged and clustered.
2. How the web supplement found new pains; signal = distinct sources.
3. How the reports were merged into T themes.
4. Quotes kept in the original language and verified verbatim ({{NQUOTES}} quotes, all pass); no usernames.
5. Dates of research and write-up.

**How to read each article:** What users are saying → Voices → Why it hurts → How people cope → Response → Open questions.

> [!warning] About "(unconfirmed)"
> Confirmed capabilities are only: <list>. Anything else in these articles is marked unconfirmed and is not a promise.

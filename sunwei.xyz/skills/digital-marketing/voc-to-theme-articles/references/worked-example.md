# Worked example: MDFriday "Voice of the Customer" (2026-09)

Inputs: 15 clusters (567 VOC cards + 592 YouTube comments), 12 new web clusters + 4 Chinese-community themes (117 archived pages). Merged into **25 themes**, one article each, plus an index. **153 quotes**, all verified verbatim. Articles were written in Chinese prose with bilingual titles; quotes stay in their original language.

## Excerpt: "Single-note Sharing" (prose translated from Chinese)

> **What users are saying.** "I just want to send this one note to someone." Publishing a whole site or setting up GitHub is too heavy; copy-paste breaks the formatting and drifts from the original. Competitors already sit here: Share Note, JotBird, Docferry, Obsius. Users care about theme and content fidelity, optional encryption, **links that don't expire**, and internal links that still work.
>
> > [!info] Signal (approximate)
> > VOC cards ~11 · YouTube comments ~9 (most mention Share Note as the better choice).
> > Rank **#15**: the smallest conversion entry point, and competitors already hold it.

> **Voices** (excerpt)
> - “Sometimes I just want to share one note with someone.” — Reddit r/ObsidianMD · 2026-04-09 · <https://www.reddit.com/r/ObsidianMD/comments/1sgom74/is-there-a-good-way-to-share-a-single-obsidian-note-without-publishing-your-whol>
> - “Problem is with expiration date for the links. That is something I cannot accept!” — YouTube comment · ~2026 · <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4t3J8m7Z_jE>

> **Product's response** (excerpt)
> - Right-click publish of a single note + AS mode fidelity + switchable themes.
> - Guest needs no sign-up, which fits this scenario best.
> - **Conflict:** Guest content is cleared at the next UTC midnight and Free on the 1st of each month, which runs straight into "expiring links are unacceptable". Say clearly: for long-lived links, use Personal (kept permanently).
> - Encrypted sharing, revoke at any time (unconfirmed).

> **Open questions.** How many Guest single-note publishers upgrade? How are internal links handled when publishing one note (unconfirmed)? Is the clearing rule visible enough on the success page?

## Lessons from the run

- Rendering from a quote bank with placeholders made "all quotes verified" a build property, not a manual check.
- The "(unconfirmed)" warning in the index lists the confirmed capabilities once, so articles can stay short.
- Use path-qualified or unique wikilinks; bare names that exist in two folders break resolution.

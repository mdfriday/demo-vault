## When to use

- Every Tuesday, **Conversations** day, with a goal of at least 5 meaningful conversations.
- Any time I want to know why users choose (or don't choose) MDFriday.

## Goal

Answer this question:

> Why would someone choose MDFriday instead of their current publishing workflow?

## Channels

- Reddit
- Obsidian Forum
- GitHub

## Steps

1. Find people who are discussing publishing pain points (for example, relevant Reddit threads).
2. Understand first; don't sell.
3. Ask three core questions:
   - How are you publishing today?
   - What is frustrating?
   - Why this workflow?
4. Record users' own words and file them in [[mdfriday/voice-of-custom/index|Voice of the Customer]].
5. Bring the findings into Wednesday's Build and Saturday's [[building/index|Building Note]].

## Output

- Conversation notes (users' own words)
- 1 Learning → possibly triggering a Product Decision

## Checklist

The measure isn't Views, but:

- [ ] Comments
- [ ] Conversations
- [ ] DM
- [ ] Follow-up discussions

This note is the procedure. There is no separate agent file.

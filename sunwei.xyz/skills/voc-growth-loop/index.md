## When to use

- Every day: find new public posts about publishing or sharing notes, and add them to the [[voc-db/README|VOC database]].
- Every week and month: check whether pages built from these voices earn impressions, clicks and installs.

## How it works

1. **Find.** Search Reddit, the Obsidian forum, HN and GitHub issues, using the target keywords from the [[seo/index|SEO strategy]] briefs and the 25 [[voice-of-custom/index|topic summaries]].
2. **Capture.** One card per public post, de-duplicated by URL. Quotes stay verbatim. Fit is judged only from confirmed product facts.
3. **Connect.** Every card links to one topic and, when it fits, to the SEO brief it supports. When several voices have no matching topic or brief, propose a new one.
4. **Engage.** Draft helpful replies for review (never post automatically), then log replies and follow-ups on the card.
5. **Prove.** For each live page, record Search Console impressions, clicks and queries plus `install_click`, and compare them with the voices behind the page. Don't change titles or main keywords during the first 8 weeks.

## Standards

- Everything is public and in English; quotes keep their original language.
- Never invent quotes, facts or numbers. If there is no data yet, say so.
- Fact priority: founder confirmation > product source code > help center > old drafts.

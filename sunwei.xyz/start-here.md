The folders are the map. A note moves when the work does.

## The Loop

1. [[building/index|Building]] records what I'm doing, learning, and thinking about right now.
2. [[knowledge/index|Knowledge]] captures lessons worth keeping, from real practice and beyond.
3. [[skills/index|Skills]] turns knowledge into reusable procedures that AI can follow.

The goal is to turn experience into knowledge, knowledge into reusable assets, and those assets into more meaningful work.

## The Folders

- [[building/index|Now]] — What I'm doing, learning, and thinking about right now.
- [[knowledge/index|Knowledge]] — Ideas and knowledge learned through practice and beyond.
- [[skills/index|Skills]] — Knowledge turned into reusable procedures that AI can follow.
- [[mdfriday/index|MDFriday]] — The product I'm building, including strategy, user feedback, and market research.
- [[books/index|Books]] — Books I'm writing.
- [[notes/index|Notes]] — Thoughts, observations, and events worth keeping.

## Knowledge Growth

Notes evolve as my understanding improves.

> [!info] Growth stages
> - `#seedling` — A rough note that isn't ready to follow.
> - `#budding` — A revised note that's still evolving.
> - `#evergreen` — A note complete enough to use, but still open to correction.

Use one growth tag per note. When a note matures, replace its previous tag with the new one.

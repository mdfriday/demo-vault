The folders are the map. A note moves when the work does.

> [!info] Growth
> - `#seedling` — a rough note, not ready to follow.
> - `#budding` — revised, still changing.
> - `#evergreen` — complete enough to use, still open to correction.
>
> One growth tag per note. The more mature tag wins if two are present.

## The loop

1. [[building/index|Now]] records what actually happened.
2. A lesson worth keeping is written into [[knowledge/index|Knowledge]].
3. A procedure I want an AI to repeat becomes a [[skills/index|Skill]], and it links back to the knowledge it came from.

## The assets

- [[mdfriday/index|MDFriday]] is the product: positioning, decisions, and research. How-to guides stay on [mdfriday.com](https://mdfriday.com).
- [[books/index|Books]] are books I write. A folder is only a book page once it has a real title and a way to read it.
- [[one-person-company/index|One Person Company]] is the practice of building that company, step by step.

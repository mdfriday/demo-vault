Experience

17 Years in Software

I've spent most of my life building software and helping teams build it.

  1. Early Years

    JavaScript foundations

    Before jQuery, I built a JavaScript Class Framework based on Prototype principles — applying object-oriented ideas to JavaScript itself.

    I was interested in understanding how things worked, not just using them.

  2. Huawei

    Networks & systems

    Worked on L2TP, PPP and internet access services — network, systems and enterprise-grade software.

    Learned how large systems are built beneath the application layer.

  3. ITOTEM

    Mobile startup years

    Joined a mobile internet startup. Built iOS products, ran a small-project business line, hired, talked to customers and closed opportunities.

    Moved from engineer to customer, sales and team building.

  4. Symbio

    Delivery & management

    Led a mobile delivery department of about 20 people across technical presales, project management, delivery and iOS development.

    Technology + sales + delivery + management.

  5. Thoughtworks

    Technology strategy

    TechOps work spanning technology strategy, best practices, transformation and executive reporting — collaborating with teams in the US, Brazil, India and China.

    Moved from software engineering toward technology strategy.

  6. Now

    Building my own business

    Putting those experiences together — using AI, software and everything I've learned to find real problems and build useful products.

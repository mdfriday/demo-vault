Foundry

An internal engine for building structured sites from content — exploring how far Hugo-like ideas can go in a TypeScript world.

Project · Engine · Experiment

Foundry is not a consumer product page. It is the technical substrate behind publishing experiments — a place to test pipelines, templates, and content models before they harden into MDFriday features.

What I'm exploring

  • Content → site pipelines with clear stages
  • Typed models for notes, pages and gardens
  • Template boundaries that stay maintainable
  • Rebuild cost and incremental publishing

Relationship to MDFriday

MDFriday is the product surface. Foundry is closer to the workshop floor. Some ideas will ship. Some will be discarded after they teach something. That separation keeps the personal brand from collapsing into a single product.

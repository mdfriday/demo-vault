MDFriday

A publishing platform for turning knowledge into websites, products and businesses.

Project · Publishing · In progress

MDFriday started from a simple problem: how can people publish what they already know without rebuilding their workflow from scratch?

It is my current product experiment — not my identity. sunwei.xyz remains the personal home; MDFriday is the software being built and tested with users.

Current focus

  • Publish Obsidian notes
  • Digital Gardens
  • Knowledge websites
  • AI-native publishing
  • Personal knowledge → public assets

Why it matters to me

After 17 years building software for other organizations, I'm testing whether I can build a sustainable one-person business around knowledge, software and AI. MDFriday is the sharpest expression of that question right now.

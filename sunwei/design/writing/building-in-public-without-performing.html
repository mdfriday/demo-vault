Building in Public Without Performing

How I'm documenting the journey of building products and finding customers without turning every update into a highlight reel.

Building · Mar 21, 2026 · 6 min read

Building in public can become performance. Views reward certainty, aesthetics, and “wins.” Real product work is slower: unclear problems, awkward demos, and weeks where the main output is learning what not to do.

What I want to show

  • Product experiments and user conversations
  • Technical decisions and tradeoffs
  • Failures that change the next step
  • The boring middle of shipping

What I refuse to fake

I don't want this site — or YouTube — to imply I've figured out the path. The honest frame is: I'm building something of my own after 17 years in software, and I'm documenting what I learn.

Keep building for the long term. Don't perform a finished identity.

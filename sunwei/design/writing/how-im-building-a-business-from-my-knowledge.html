How I'm Building a Business From My Knowledge

The experiment of turning expertise into products, users and revenue — documented as it happens, not as a finished playbook.

Building · Feb 9, 2026 · 8 min read

The question I'm testing is simple: Can I build a sustainable business around what I know?

Not “teach people how to make money.” Not “package motivation.” A practical loop: find a real problem, build a small solution, talk to users, sell it, learn, repeat.

The loop

  1. Find a real problem
  2. Build a small solution
  3. Talk to real users
  4. Sell it
  5. Learn from what happens
  6. Repeat

Why MDFriday fits this question

Knowledge workers already create notes, essays, and systems of thought. The hard part is often publishing and owning that work as a public asset. MDFriday is my attempt to meet that problem where people already are — starting with Obsidian and Markdown.

I'm building. I haven't figured it all out.

What this site is for

sunwei.xyz is the place I own for thinking and tracking the journey. YouTube is where I show the process. MDFriday is the product experiment. They are related — but they are not the same thing.

I am Mira Cho. I am 25, and I work as a research engineer on retrieval for models that have to run on a laptop, not a cluster. The other half of the week is plants: a calathea, a pothos that forgives everything, and a fern I named Draft because it keeps collapsing.

This garden is the public version of that split. Lab notes and plant logs live in different folders, then point at each other when the analogy is actually useful. [[the-windowsill]] and [[retrieval-as-light]] are the two I send people first.

Readers can subscribe at the bottom of any page. When I want to write to them, I export the list into this vault and answer from here. The garden stays the address book.

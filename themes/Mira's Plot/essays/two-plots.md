People ask which one is the hobby. Neither. Retrieval notes go stale the way fronds go brown: slowly, then all at once, if you only look on Sundays.

[[retrieval-as-light]] and [[the-windowsill]] are featured from different folders because a visitor should see both on the way in. The stream in [[now/index|Now]] is where they mix, one short entry at a time, without pretending each day is an essay.

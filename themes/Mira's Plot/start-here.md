Welcome. I am Mira Cho. I work on retrieval for small models, and I keep a windowsill of plants that are better at patience than I am. This site is the public half of that notebook.

## Growth, as tags

Every note carries a stage. They are ordinary Obsidian tags. The most mature one wins.

- `#seedling` — planted today, still muddy. See [[still-fuzzy]].
- `#budding` — rewritten a few times, still changing. See [[the-windowsill]].
- `#evergreen` — solid enough to rely on, still open to a later edit. See [[publish-while-it-grows]].

A note with no stage tag is treated as a seedling. That is intentional. Rough work should look rough.

Featured cards on the home page are not one folder. A root note, a lab note, a plant note, and an essay can sit in the same row. I mark those with `featured: true` wherever they live.

## Publish before it is done

A blog post waits until the argument is closed. A garden note goes out when it is true enough to be useful, then gets revised in public. [[publish-while-it-grows]] is the longer version of that.

## What a note can hold

Wikilinks, LaTeX, Mermaid, and an Obsidian base all render here. [[retrieval-as-light]] has the formula, [[a-small-architecture]] has the diagram, and [[the-bench]] lists the lab with a base block.

If you want new notes by email, the subscribe form is on this page. I export that list back into the vault when I write to readers. I do not keep a separate spreadsheet.
